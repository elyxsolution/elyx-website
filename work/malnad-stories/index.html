The brief

What they needed.

Malnad Stories turns trek and travel photographs into printed albums. That is more than a catalogue site: customers upload their own photos, design an album, pay online and expect it to arrive as a finished product.

The platform had to handle uploads and storage, custom printing orders and secure payments, and still feel simple to a customer who just wants their memories on a shelf.

What we built

What shipped.

01

Custom printing flow

Customers build their album from their own photos and place a print order within the same experience.

02

Secure payments

Online checkout built around secure payment handling for Indian customers.

03

Cloud storage for photos

Reliable storage for customers' photographs, so uploads and orders are safe and quick to retrieve.

04

Full-stack, launch ready

Front end, back end, database and admin tooling built by one team and delivered launch ready.

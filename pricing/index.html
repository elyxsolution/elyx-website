Questions

Pricing questions.

What is the minimum I can spend with Elyx?

Engagements start at ₹9,999 for a focused landing page or redesign, and that is the starting price across every service. Final pricing scales with scope, and we always quote it up front in writing.

Do you charge by the hour?

No. We price by outcome, not by the hour. You get a fixed scope, timeline and price before any work begins, with no surprise line items mid-project.

How do payments work?

50% upfront to begin and 50% on final delivery, covered by a straightforward statement of work with scope, deliverables, timeline and revision rounds. No retainer lock-ins unless you want one.

Is GST included in the price?

Prices on this site are in INR and exclusive of taxes. GST is added at the applicable rate (currently 18% for software services) on the invoice.

What does the Launch tier include?

Discovery and scoping, design and build of one service, launch and handover, and 30 days of post-launch support.

What if my project grows after we start?

Changes to scope are discussed and priced before we do them, so nothing is added to the bill silently. Many clients start with the Launch tier and move to Build or Partner as the project grows.

Can I get a rough number before a call?

Yes. Use the website cost calculator for an indicative range in a minute, then we confirm it properly in writing.

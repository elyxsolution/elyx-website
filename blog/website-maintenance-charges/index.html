Website Maintenance Cost in India: What You Pay Monthly and Why

Website maintenance charges in India: monthly ranges by site type, the yearly renewals that surprise owners, a worked annual budget and what to ask first.

Website maintenance charges in India run from a few hundred rupees a month for a static brochure site to over a lakh for an enterprise site, and Aarav Infotech's 2026 guide puts most small and mid-size business sites between ₹2,500 and ₹20,000 a month. The qualification is that the monthly fee is rarely the whole bill. Domain, hosting and SSL renewals often arrive once a year, separately, and that invoice is the one that surprises owners.

  • Monthly ranges. Two Indian guides read in November 2026 agree on the shape (cheap static sites, mid-priced business sites, dearer stores) but disagree sharply on e-commerce.
  • Renewals. Domain, hosting and SSL bill yearly and often sit outside the monthly fee, so ask what a quote leaves out.
  • Content hours. Plans cap included edits, and the overage is where a cheap plan turns expensive.
  • Tax. GST and TDS treatment depends on your contract, so confirm it with a chartered accountant; this page gives no tax advice.

What Website Maintenance Charges Cover

Website maintenance charges pay for six things: hosting, updates, backups, security, content changes and the renewals that keep the site online. They bill in different rhythms, which is why one quote can look cheap and another expensive.

ItemWhat you getHow it usually bills
HostingServer space and uptime upkeepYearly renewal, or monthly
UpdatesCMS, plugin and theme patchesMonthly, inside the plan
BackupsScheduled copies of files and databaseMonthly, or bundled with hosting
SecurityMalware scans, firewall, login protectionMonthly, inside the plan
Content changesText, image and price editsCapped hours per month
RenewalsDomain, SSL, paid plugins, email, APIsYearly, often separate

The services come from the lists published by Aarav Infotech (2026) and ARE InfoTech (its page is labelled 2024). The billing column is my reading of how those firms and the plans below describe them.

Aarav also separates maintenance from website management, meaning SEO, content marketing and ad management, which it prices as a different service. A quote that bundles both cannot be compared with one that does not.

Hosting, Domain and SSL: The Renewals That Surprise Owners

The renewals that surprise owners are the yearly ones, because hosting, domain, SSL and paid extras sit outside most monthly fees. ARE InfoTech lists the fixed items as domain renewal, SSL renewal, hosting renewal, email hosting, paid plugins or CMS licences, and third-party services such as an SMS or payment gateway.

For hosting, Aarav Infotech gives yearly bands for India: shared ₹3,500 to ₹18,000, VPS ₹5,000 to ₹50,000 and dedicated ₹50,000 to ₹3,00,000 and up. Network Solutions warns that domain renewal prices often rise after a cheap first year.

GoDaddy's own hosting page advertises a free domain, email and SSL for one year. My reading: year two is then the first full-price bill, which is the moment many owners notice.

Redpulse advises checking whether a yearly quote includes hosting and domain renewal, and says that difference explains most of the gap between two similar quotes. Copy this renewal checklist and fill it once:

  • [ ] Domain: renewal date, registrar, and whose name and email it sits under
  • [ ] Hosting: the renewal price (not the intro price), the plan type and who holds the login
  • [ ] SSL: free or paid, and who renews it
  • [ ] Business email: cost per mailbox per year
  • [ ] Paid plugins, themes and licences: the renewal month for each
  • [ ] Third-party services: SMS, payment gateway, delivery APIs

Redpulse reports that in its own audit of 333 solar installers, 21% of the companies that owned a domain had let it lapse into a parking page or a dead site. That is one sector and one firm's audit, but it shows why renewal dates belong on a calendar.

Updates, Backups and Security: What the Monthly Fee Buys

The monthly fee mostly buys attention: someone applies updates, checks backups and watches security so a problem is found before a customer finds it. Aarav says an unpatched plugin remains one of the most common ways into a WordPress site, and that a backup is only proven once someone has restored it.

Redpulse's three tiers show how the same work scales. The table lists its published inclusions, last reviewed 2 August 2026.

TierBackupsSecurityResponse
BasicWeeklyMonthly patchesEmail, 48 hours
StandardDailyWeekly scans, malware removalEmail and phone, 24 hours
PremiumHourlyReal-time monitoring, firewallSame-day emergency

Redpulse says custom builds need less frequent patching than WordPress but need specialist skill, which is why its custom band starts higher. The takeaway is that backup frequency and response time, not the word "maintenance", are what separate a low price from a high one.

Content Changes: Where a Cheap Plan Gets Expensive

A cheap plan gets expensive at the content line, because most plans include only a few edits or hours a month and bill the rest. Aarav says included hours are typically 2 to 5 a month, and Redpulse's Basic tier includes 2 content changes and its Standard tier 5.

Here is the arithmetic with assumed inputs. A plan includes 2 hours. You send a new price list, an offer banner, two photos and a holiday notice, which takes 5 hours.

  • Extra hours a month: 5 minus 2 is 3.
  • Extra hours a year: 3 times 12 is 36.
  • Cost of the overage: 36 times the hourly rate written in your quote.

None of those hours is measured; replace them with your own last three months of requests. Redpulse says a site updated weekly needs its Standard or Premium tier. If you only change a few prices, a CMS (a dashboard where you edit text yourself) can remove the line altogether.

Website Maintenance Cost Per Year: A Worked Example

A small business site on a Standard-tier plan costs about ₹39,500 to ₹1,02,000 a year before tax in this example, because the plan bills monthly and hosting renews separately. The inputs are Redpulse's Standard band and Aarav's shared-hosting band, so this is a method with example numbers, not a forecast.

  1. Plan. ₹3,000 times 12 is ₹36,000, and ₹7,000 times 12 is ₹84,000.
  2. Hosting, if not included. Add ₹3,500 to ₹18,000 for a year of shared hosting.
  3. Total before tax. ₹36,000 plus ₹3,500 is ₹39,500, and ₹84,000 plus ₹18,000 is ₹1,02,000.
  4. Tax illustration. At 18% (the rate that applies to software services), ₹39,500 gives ₹7,110 and a total of ₹46,610; ₹1,02,000 gives ₹18,360 and ₹1,20,360.

If your quote already includes hosting, drop step 2. Domain, paid plugins and email are not in the total, so add them from your renewal checklist. Which lines carry GST is a question for your accountant.

Website Maintenance Charges: TDS, GST and HSN Code

Whether TDS applies to website maintenance charges depends on your contract and who is paying, and the research behind this article holds no primary text from the Income Tax Department, so it states no rate or threshold. This is a business lens, not tax advice, and it reflects what was available on 17 November 2026.

What the sources do support is narrow. Elyx's prices are exclusive of GST, currently 18% on software services, added on the invoice. One search result for the TDS query was titled as an explainer on Section 194J (TDS on professional and technical fees), but it was not read, so treat it as a lead for your accountant, not an answer.

Ask your chartered accountant three things:

  • Do we deduct TDS on this payment, under which section and above what threshold?
  • Which service classification (HSN or SAC) code should the invoice carry?
  • Which lines of the quote attract GST?

Then confirm each answer on the official Income Tax Department and GST portal pages. Rules change, so recheck before each renewal.

What Usually Goes Wrong With Maintenance Quotes

Most maintenance disputes come from scope the quote never stated: hours, response times, logins and tests. The causes below come from Aarav and Redpulse, who sell maintenance, so the fixes are written from the buyer's side.

ProblemCauseFix
"Unlimited changes"No defined scope, so turnaround slows (Redpulse)Get a quota in hours
Overage surpriseIncluded hours capped at 2 to 5 (Aarav)Cost the overage in hours first
Provider holds your loginsHosting, domain or CMS admin in their name (Aarav)Register in your name, keep admin
Backups never testedNo verification step (Redpulse)Ask for the last restore date
No response promiseResponse time drives cost (Aarav)Get hours-to-respond in writing
One freelancerNo cover when they are away (Aarav)Ask who covers an incident
Neglect, then a hackCleanup quoted at ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 by both firmsBudget the plan, not the cleanup

The cleanup figure is a vendor's own quote, not independent data, and Aarav adds that sites neglected for a year often need a rebuild. Aarav also puts freelancer fees at ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 a month against ₹15,000 to ₹20,000 for an agency on the same work, a gap that pays for redundancy.

Do You Need a Monthly Plan? A Decision Guide

You need a monthly plan when the site changes often, takes payments or enquiries, and nobody on your team can fix a broken form. This framework is my own, built from Redpulse's four questions and Aarav's comparison of DIY, freelancer and agency.

  • Choose DIY when the site is static, with no CMS, plugins or payments, and you can use a hosting panel. Aarav puts that at roughly ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 a year for domain and hosting.
  • Choose a freelancer for a low-stakes WordPress site, and accept that one person is a single point of failure.
  • Choose an agency or a care plan when the site takes bookings, payments or leads, because response time and cover matter more than the monthly price.
  • Choose a redesign first when the site is three to four years old or no longer brings enquiries, since Redpulse notes that maintenance cannot fix an underperforming site.

One test settles close calls. If an hour of downtime costs you more than a month of maintenance, buy the higher tier.

What to Do Next

Start by filling the renewal checklist above, then ask every provider the same five questions in writing. They are: the patching schedule and the time allowed for a critical fix, whether you get a monthly report, who owns the hosting, domain and admin accounts, how many content hours are included and the rate beyond them, and the response time if the site is down or hacked.

Next, run the annual arithmetic with your own quote. The pricing page explains how Elyx quotes build costs; the care ranges above cover upkeep.

If you want the studio to look after a site, whether it built it or not, the maintenance and care page is the place to start with a health check. A fixed quote is confirmed in writing after a call.

Frequently asked questions

How much does website maintenance cost per month in India?

Most small and mid-size business sites cost between ₹2,500 and ₹20,000 a month, according to Aarav Infotech's 2026 guide. Redpulse Software's bands run from ₹1,500 for basic sites to ₹15,000 and up for stores and web apps. Elyx quotes care plans to your requirements, exclusive of GST, with engagements starting from ₹9,999.

What are website maintenance charges?

Website maintenance charges are the recurring fees for keeping a site online, secure and current. They cover hosting, updates, backups, security checks and a set number of content changes, plus yearly renewals for domain and SSL. Aarav Infotech treats SEO and content marketing as a separate service.

Does it cost money to maintain a website?

Yes, even a simple site has yearly costs for a domain and hosting. Aarav Infotech puts the do-it-yourself minimum for a static site at about ₹3,000 to ₹5,000 a year. Sites with a CMS, plugins or payments also need updates, backups and security work, whether you pay someone or do it yourself.

How much should I charge for website maintenance?

Price it by scope, not by a flat figure: the site type, hosting tier, response time and included content hours. Aarav Infotech reports freelancers at ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 a month and agencies at ₹15,000 to ₹20,000 for the same work, and the gap is mostly cover and response. Write the hours and response time into the quote.

Is TDS applicable on website maintenance charges?

It can depend on the contract and the payer, and this article cannot give a rate because the research holds no primary Income Tax Department text. Ask your chartered accountant whether you deduct TDS, under which section and above what threshold. Check the answer against the official Income Tax Department pages.

What is the website maintenance cost per year in India?

A small business site on a standard plan comes to about ₹39,500 to ₹1,02,000 a year before tax in the worked example above. That example adds Redpulse's Standard band times 12 to Aarav Infotech's shared-hosting band, and 18% GST would come on top. Treat it as a method with example inputs, not a forecast.

What happens if I do not maintain my website?

Plugins and software go out of date, which Aarav Infotech calls one of the most common ways attackers get into WordPress sites. Both Aarav and Redpulse quote ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 or more to clean up a hacked site, and neglected sites sometimes need a rebuild. Those are vendor quotes, not independent data.

Is hosting included in website maintenance charges?

Not always, so ask. Redpulse says whether a yearly quote includes hosting and domain renewal explains most of the gap between two similar quotes. The studio's listed care-plan inclusions do not mention hosting, so confirm it in the written quote.

Wedding Planners and Event Companies: Websites That Handle Peak-Season Enquiries

What an event management company website needs for peak season: packages, captioned galleries, enquiry routing and reviews, with a worked example.

An event management company website handles peak-season enquiries when a wedding family or a corporate buyer can state the date, city and guest count in one step, when packages are visible instead of hidden behind "contact us", and when galleries and reviews prove the work. We read four event-company sites (Craftworld Events, MadRun Communications, Chennai Events Management and Events & Pro), and none states a price or a package list in the text we extracted. The honest qualification is that the research holds no data on Indian wedding-season volumes, so the peak-season argument below is my reasoning, shown with assumed numbers you should replace with your own.

  • Packages. Name who each package is for, what it includes and excludes, and how the price is set, even if the rupee figure waits for a call.
  • Galleries. Caption every photo with the event type, city, guest count and what your team handled.
  • Routing. Send wedding families and corporate buyers down different enquiry paths, and capture the date first.
  • Reviews. Use reviews a stranger can check: named, dated, and on Google as well as on your page.

Event Management Company Website: What the Page Must Do

An event management company website must turn a first visit into a dated, qualified enquiry, because an event has a fixed day and the buyer is usually comparing several firms. The studio's web development page says most small-business sites fail in three places: they load slowly on a phone, they bury the one action a visitor should take, and the owner cannot update them. For an event firm, the one action is "tell us your date, city and guest count".

The same page lists enquiry paths as part of a build: a contact form, WhatsApp click-to-chat, a call button and Google Maps, tracked by source. Elyx Solutions' web development service covers sites of this kind.

Peak season changes the design, in my reading. MadRun's page describes on-site event coordination and Chennai Events Management's describes 24/7 on-site service, so the people who would answer the phone are often standing in a venue. The site has to collect enough to reply well without a call.

Packages: Show the Structure Even When the Price Waits

Packages work on an event website when each one states its audience, inclusions, exclusions and how its price is set. Chennai Events Management says it builds plans around "vision and budget", and Events & Pro says its work suits complex logistics, which signals a scope band but gives no way to compare before a call.

MadRun's service list shows the blocks event firms already sell: stage fabrication, LED walls, lighting, sound, décor, entertainment, photography and coordination. Packages are those blocks grouped for a type of buyer. The table is my framework, not a sourced standard.

Field on each packageWhy it earns its placeHow to write it
Who it is forStops the wrong buyer enquiringA guest-count band and event type
IncludedLets a family compare you with a rivalBlocks from your own service list
Not includedPrevents the "I thought that was covered" callVenue, catering or travel, if you do not handle them
How the price is setFilters on budget before your time is spentPer guest, per function or flat, and "from" your own figure
Lead time and next stepProtects your calendar in peak weeksMonths of notice, then "send date, city and guest count"

Choose a "from" price when your packages repeat and you can stand behind the floor. Choose a scope band and a request-a-proposal button when every event is custom, as with large corporate work. Keep the text editable: the studio's web development page describes a CMS so prices, photos and text change without touching code.

Galleries That Prove the Work and Still Load on a Phone

A gallery proves the work when every photo says what the event was, where, how big and what your team handled. Events & Pro captions images with the event theme and city, such as a partnership event at a Pune venue or an astrology event in Delhi. MadRun's page carries one project line naming scope and headcount: a resort event near Mysore for more than 200 employees, bundling travel, stay, a DJ party, a dance troupe and an emcee.

A caption with scope and size answers whether you have run something like theirs. Write one line per photo set:

  • Event type and city. "Wedding reception, Mysuru" tells a family more than "Event 14".
  • Guest count. It shows scale in one number.
  • What you handled. Name the blocks: stage, sound, décor, coordination.
  • Permission. Get written clearance from the client and venue first, a practice I recommend and not a rule I am citing.

Give weddings, corporate and social events their own gallery pages, so a bride's father and a marketing head do not scroll past each other's content. On speed, the studio's facts say live Instagram feeds can slow a site, so it recommends curated images, and its real-estate page stresses serving heavy image sets fast for buyers on phones.

Enquiry Routing: Weddings and Corporate Buyers Need Different Paths

Enquiry routing works when the site asks each buyer different first questions and sends the enquiry to the person who can answer fast. MadRun and Chennai Events Management both serve weddings and corporate clients under one general pitch, while Events & Pro serves corporate buyers only and gives lead times in its FAQ. For its own work, Events & Pro says to start 4 to 6 months ahead for summits of 1,000 or more delegates or complex launches, and 2 to 3 months ahead for executive retreats or custom galas.

Those lead times are one company's guidance for corporate work, not an industry rule or a wedding figure. They show why the date comes first: it tells you how urgent the enquiry is. The table is my suggested routing.

Enquiry typeCapture firstReply goes toReply by
Wedding or family functionDate, city or venue, guest count, functionsPlanner on the wedding calendarA time you can keep, stated on the page
Corporate or MICEDate window, delegates, city, event type, work emailCorporate leadSame working day, call offered
Social (birthday, baby shower)Date, city, guest countOwner of small eventsNext working day

Keep the form to about six fields, date first. Add a WhatsApp button that opens a pre-filled message, a click-to-chat pattern the studio's restaurant FAQ describes:

Hi, I am planning a [wedding / corporate event] in [city] around [date] for about [guests] guests. Could you share your availability and a package outline?

Where the enquiry lands matters as much as the form. The studio's real-estate FAQ says leads can go to WhatsApp, email, a Google Sheet or a CRM (a tool that keeps enquiries in one list) with alerts so none are missed, and its coaching FAQ describes GA4 events for form submissions, WhatsApp clicks and calls. Send each enquiry to two people, so a planner on site does not hold the only copy. For a date-hold advance, the salon booking article shows what vendors state about deposits and notes that none of the pages it read documented UPI, so confirm that with your payment tool.

Worked Example: One Peak Week of Enquiries

A structured form can save hours in a peak week, and the arithmetic below shows how to test that with your own numbers. Every input is an assumption, not a measurement and not a client result.

  • Assumed week: 36 enquiries arrive.
  • By phone, no form: about 12 minutes each to learn date, city, guests and budget. That is 36 times 12, or 432 minutes, which is 7.2 hours.
  • With a form capturing those items: about 4 minutes each to read and reply. That is 36 times 4, or 144 minutes, which is 2.4 hours.
  • Saving: 432 minus 144 is 288 minutes, or 4.8 hours.

Now stress it. If a third of the enquiries, 12 of 36, arrive incomplete and need an 8-minute callback, the extra is 12 times 8, or 96 minutes. The total becomes 144 plus 96, or 240 minutes, and the saving drops to 432 minus 240, or 192 minutes, about 3.2 hours.

Count your own enquiries for two weeks and time the callbacks. If the saving is under an hour a week, a WhatsApp quick reply is probably enough.

Reviews: Proof a Buyer Can Check

Reviews persuade an event buyer when the reviewer, event type and date can be checked, and when they also appear on Google, not only on your own page. Craftworld Events shows three testimonials attributed to named people with company roles. Chennai Events Management shows four unattributed ones covering a product launch, a corporate retreat, a wedding and a birthday.

The Chennai set spreads across event types, but nobody in it can be asked. My reading is that one attributed, dated review of a specific event beats four unnamed paragraphs.

The studio's SEO page lists a Business Profile and a reviews process as part of local SEO. Build a simple habit around it:

  1. Within a week of the event, send a WhatsApp thank-you with your Business Profile review link.
  2. Ask which event the client is reviewing, so the review names it.
  3. Publish permitted quotes with the client's name, event type and month.
  4. Reply to every review, including critical ones, in plain words.

Never write reviews for yourself or pay for them, and keep every claim provable.

Event Management Company Website Template or a Custom Build?

A template is enough when your offer is simple and one person handles enquiries, and a custom build earns its cost when packages, routing and galleries must work as one system. As of 16 November 2026, searching "event management company website template" returns pages from Squarespace and Webflow, and free lists such as Gevme's five free templates for event planners. I read only the result titles and tested no template, so I make no claim about what one includes.

A template gives you a layout, not packages, captions, routing or reviews.

  • Choose a template when: you serve one city, offer two or three packages and answer enquiries yourself.
  • Choose a custom build when: you serve weddings and corporate buyers, need a CMS for packages and galleries, or want enquiries tracked by source.

Event Management Company Near Me: Local Search and Google Maps

Searches such as "event management company near me" are won on Google Maps and your Business Profile as much as on your home page. The studio's SEO answer says that for a business with a physical location, the Business Profile, reviews and consistent address and phone details matter as much as the website. Three of the four pages we read put a city in the heading: Mumbai, Bengaluru and Chennai.

Match your name, address and phone number exactly between your profile and your site, and embed the map on the contact page. Make a page for each city and event type where you really work, as the studio's SEO facts recommend pages per service and per customer type. MadRun's page claims events across Bengaluru and Karnataka, the kind of stated reach to copy.

The Google Business Profile checker is a quick first pass. Google's Business Profile Help Center holds the service-area and review rules, and this research did not include it, so read it first.

Most event-site failures come from one generic path for every buyer, thin proof and slow handling of peak-week enquiries, not from weak design. The causes and fixes are my reading of the pages we read and the studio's facts.

What to Do Next

Fix the date field and one package page first, then add captions and reviews, and measure enquiries for a fortnight. Copy this checklist:

  • [ ] The first field on every enquiry form is the event date.
  • [ ] Wedding, corporate and social enquiries have separate paths.
  • [ ] Each package states audience, inclusions, exclusions, price method and lead time.
  • [ ] Every gallery photo carries event type, city, guest count and scope.
  • [ ] Reviews carry a name, an event and a month, and the same ones sit on Google.
  • [ ] Enquiries alert two people, and form, WhatsApp and call events are tracked.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create an event management website?

Start with the one action a visitor takes, which is sending a date, city and guest count. Then build a services page, package pages, captioned galleries, reviews and a contact page with a form and a WhatsApp button. A template can carry the layout, but the packages, captions and routing rules have to be yours.

What is corporate event management?

Corporate event management is the planning and running of business events for a company. MadRun's page lists conferences, seminars, exhibitions, award nights, dealer meets, product launches, offsites and team-building. Events & Pro adds MICE (meetings, incentives, conferences and exhibitions).

What do event management companies do?

Event management companies plan, design and run events from concept to on-site coordination. MadRun lists venue planning, stage fabrication, LED walls, lighting, sound, décor, entertainment, photography and coordination. Chennai Events Management adds catering and logistics for weddings, receptions and birthdays.

How should an event company website show packages?

Show each package with its audience, inclusions, exclusions, how the price is set and the lead time needed. Use a "from" price only if you can stand behind it, and a scope band with a proposal request for custom work. None of the four sites we read states a price in the extracted text.

How early do clients enquire for events?

The research gives one sourced figure, for corporate work only. Events & Pro recommends 4 to 6 months for summits of 1,000 or more delegates and complex launches, and 2 to 3 months for executive retreats and custom galas. There is no wedding figure, so use your own booking history.

How much does an event management company website cost?

Elyx engagements start from ₹9,999, excluding GST. A landing page and a business website of 5 to 10 pages are each quoted to your requirements, in writing, after a call. The pricing page explains how.

How do I start an event management company?

This article covers the website, and the research has no primary source on setting up an event business, so it gives no steps. The pages we read show firms choosing a scope, such as corporate only or weddings and corporate together, so decide yours first. Confirm business structure and tax with a chartered accountant.

How do I register an event management company?

Registration depends on how you structure the business, and the research has no primary source on it. Ask a chartered accountant which structure, tax registration and local licences apply, and check the official government portals for current rules. Rules change, so confirm them on the day you apply.

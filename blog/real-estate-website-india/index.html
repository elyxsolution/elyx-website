Real Estate Websites in India: Listings, Lead Capture and the RERA Details to Show

Real estate website India guide: project pages, virtual tours, lead routing and the RERA details to show, with what the research could not confirm.

A real estate website in India works when each project has its own page, the RERA registration number sits where a buyer can check it, photos and tours load on a phone, and every enquiry reaches one named person. Portals such as MagicBricks and 99acres bring traffic, but only your own site keeps the enquiry, the wording and the buyer's trust in your hands. The qualification that matters is regulation: the research behind this article held no primary text of the RERA Act or any state rules, so the RERA section is a business checklist, not legal advice.

  • Project pages. One page per project, built in a fixed order, ending with the registration number and a last-updated date.
  • RERA. Show the number and the issuing state authority on every page that advertises the project, and confirm the exact rules with that authority.
  • Tours. A virtual tour should be a button that loads on demand, never a block that slows the page.
  • Lead routing. Decide before launch who sees each enquiry, who replies and what happens when nobody does. None of the five pages we read covers this.

Why a Real Estate Website in India Needs More Than a Portal Listing

A portal listing rents you attention, while your own site keeps the enquiry, the wording and the buyer's contact details with you. Beegru, a rival property portal, reports in its review (read 16 November 2026) that MagicBricks leads on traffic with over 30 million monthly visitors and 1.8 million active listings. That is the scale you are competing against for attention.

The same review says one enquiry on the biggest portals can bring dozens of broker calls within a day. That is a rival's claim, not a measured rate, so read it as buyer mood.

The 33acres listing rules (read 16 November 2026) show the other side. The portal says it does not verify ownership documents, and that a paid listing does not guarantee leads, a sale or a ranking. Elyx's own real estate page says its audience is independent agents, brokers and small builders who want leads to come to them directly, not only through portals.

Project Pages: The Order Buyers Need

A project page should answer, in this order, what is being built, where, in which configurations, at what price and under which registration. The table is my page plan, built from the six blocks Elyx's real estate page lists plus two trust blocks I add. It is analysis, not study data.

BlockWhat goes in itWhy it is there
HeroProject name, locality, one Enquire buttonSays what and where at once
HighlightsAmenities, configurations, possession planLets a buyer shortlist
Floor plansOne plan per configurationMatches the buyer's budget
GalleryReal photos and clearly labelled rendersBuilds confidence
LocationMap, landmarks, connectivityElyx notes buyers shortlist by area
RERA blockNumber, authority, check dateShows the project is registered
EnquiryShort form plus WhatsAppCaptures the buyer
Last updatedDate availability was checkedStops stale pages

Elyx's page says each project page should be able to rank on its own, so give every project its own address, title and description. Accuracy rules from the 33acres policy are a good floor even for your own site: correct location, accurate pricing, a clear description, genuine images and updated availability. It also bars listing an already sold property as available.

The last-updated line borrows an idea from the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites (GIGW 3.0), which ask government sites to show when content was posted or last reviewed. GIGW does not bind a private builder, but a date on each project page tells a buyer the unit count is not months old.

Showing RERA Registration on a Real Estate Website

Show the project's RERA registration number on every page that advertises the project, next to the name of the state authority that issued it. Elyx's real estate page says advertising is regulated under RERA, including displaying the number, and its FAQ says projects covered by RERA need the number in advertising. Both tell readers to confirm requirements with their state authority. That is the only rule statement in the research, and it is a studio's summary, not the Act.

I checked the research on 16 November 2026. Here is what it documents and what is my reading.

Documented in the research

  • Elyx says registration numbers are expected on project advertising and that it builds a clear place for them.
  • PropTiger, per Beegru, lists RERA registration status among its project details.
  • A Pranshi Infra project page answers "What is the RERA registration number?" in its FAQ with a state-prefixed code.

My reading, not a legal rule

  • Use a free-text field, because numbers come from different state authorities and formats may differ.
  • Add the authority's name and a check date beside the number.
  • Enter the number from the registration certificate, never from memory or a brochure.
  • Update or pull the page if a registration lapses or the project changes.

Not covered by the research

  • The Act's text, which projects it covers and how placement and format are set.
  • How agents and brokers must register or display their own details.
  • Penalties, and differences between states.

Read the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 and your state RERA authority's site, and ask a lawyer before launch. The studio builds the field; you supply and verify the number.

Virtual Tours and Heavy Media on a Phone

A virtual tour earns its place only if it opens quickly on a phone and the project page still works without it. Beegru says MagicBricks offers AR and VR tours on select properties and Housing.com offers 3D tours, so buyers have met the format. Elyx's page lists fast galleries and walkthroughs as a need because buyers on phones leave before heavy images load.

The Studio Facts do not say Elyx produces 3D or VR tours, so assume the tour comes from you or a tour provider and the site embeds it. Confirm that on the first call. These five checks are my own:

  • Show a still image with a "View tour" button, and load the tour only on a tap.
  • Test on mobile data, not office Wi-Fi.
  • Keep a photo gallery and floor plans for buyers who skip the tour.
  • Label renders as renders, since 33acres requires genuine images of the property.
  • Add the tour link to the WhatsApp reply so a lead can open it in one tap.

Lead Routing: Who Sees Each Enquiry and Who Replies

Lead routing is the rule that decides, before the enquiry arrives, who sees it, who replies and what happens if nobody does. None of the five top pages in the research covers it, which is the gap this section fills. Elyx's page says leads can go to WhatsApp, email, a Google Sheet or a CRM, with alerts so none are missed.

A worked example, with invented numbers: a builder has two projects, each sold in 2 BHK and 3 BHK. That is 2 projects x 2 configurations = 4 routing rules, plus 1 out-of-hours rule, so 5 rules in total. Writing them on one page before launch prevents the usual failure, a shared inbox nobody owns.

Track where leads come from. Elyx's SEO page describes GA4 events for calls, WhatsApp clicks and form submissions, and its coaching page says events can be tagged by page and campaign. No source in the research gives an ideal reply time, so set a target you can keep and measure it monthly.

Buyer Data: What to Promise About a Phone Number

A builder's site can make one promise the big portals struggle to: one named person, and no passing the number on. Beegru's review says large portals share a buyer's details with several brokers after one enquiry, and the 33acres rules bar using its built-in CRM for spam, harassment or selling lead data. The 33acres page also tells users to follow applicable data protection laws.

Elyx's page for professional firms mentions India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act. The research held no text of the Act, so I state none of its duties here. Read it on the Government of India's official site, as checked on 16 November 2026, and ask a lawyer how it applies to your enquiry forms.

A consent line you could adapt, written by me: "We will use your number only to reply about [project]. One person from our team will call or message you." Pair it with a short privacy page, named staff who see enquiries, and a way to ask for deletion.

What to Do Next: A Six-Step Plan

Start with the registration certificate, because the RERA block, the page and the form all depend on it.

  1. Collect the facts. Certificate, authority name, project details and current availability.
  2. Write the routing sheet. Projects x configurations, plus an out-of-hours rule.
  3. Draft the consent line and have a professional review it and your advertising claims.
  4. Decide the tour. Who makes it, where it is hosted and how it loads.
  5. Choose the type from the table and set a review date for every number.
  6. Test on a phone over mobile data, send yourself an enquiry and time the reply.

Frequently asked questions

What is a real estate website?

A real estate website is a site that shows properties or projects and captures buyer enquiries. It can be a portal with thousands of listings, or one builder's or agent's own site. This article is about the second kind.

Which real estate website is the most accurate?

No source in the research measured accuracy, so no portal can be named. Pranshi Infra reports fake or misleading listings as a common complaint on 99acres, and Beegru says verification matters more than listing count. On your own site, accuracy comes from a named owner and a last-updated date.

Do I need to show a RERA number on my real estate website?

For projects covered by RERA, Elyx's real estate page says yes, in advertising. That is a studio's summary, not the Act. Confirm what applies to your project with your state RERA authority.

What is the RERA registration number?

It is the number a state authority gives a registered project. Pranshi Infra's project page shows one in its FAQ, beginning with letters for the state. Copy it from the certificate and show the authority beside it.

What should a real estate agent website include?

It should include a page per property or project, a short qualifying form, WhatsApp on every page and a map. Elyx lists those as the needs of agents, brokers and small builders. Whether agents must show their own registration is not covered here, so ask your state authority.

How much does a real estate website cost in India?

Elyx engagements start from ₹9,999, excluding GST, and a single-project page or a business website is quoted to your requirements. A full listings site is quoted after discovery. A fixed quote is confirmed in writing after a call.

How long does it take to build a real estate website?

On the same data, a landing page typically takes 1–2 weeks and a business website 2–4 weeks. A custom web app, the nearest line for a listings site, takes 4–8 weeks. The timeline is agreed before work starts.

Can leads go to WhatsApp or a CRM?

Yes. Elyx's real estate page says leads can go to WhatsApp, email, a Google Sheet or a CRM, with alerts so none are missed. Decide who owns each reply before launch.

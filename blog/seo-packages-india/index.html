SEO packages in India are published at anything from ₹3,000 to ₹1,50,000 or more a month, according to five agency pages I read on 19 November 2026. A monthly package should include an audit, technical fixes, on-page work, local or content work and a report on enquiries, not only a count of links. The qualification is that every figure comes from the agency selling it, so there is no independent price benchmark, and no agency can honestly promise a ranking.

  • The spread. Published entry prices run from ₹3,000 to ₹20,000 a month, and the top plans reach ₹1,00,000 and above.
  • The tax. Two of the five pages say 18% GST is added to the listed price.
  • The test. Judge a package by deliverables you can check and results you can count, not by keyword or backlink totals.
  • The limit. Every rupee figure here is another company's, and Elyx scopes ongoing SEO to your site after an audit.

Why Published SEO Prices Differ by 50 Times

SEO price in India moves with five things: the size of your site, how competitive your searches are, how many locations you target, how much content is written and how much link work is done. WebHopers names keywords, competition, content and link building. Savit names site size, competitive landscape, locations and scope.

The gap is wide: ₹1,50,000 divided by ₹3,000 is 50, so the top of Savit's range is 50 times LeadNexa's entry plan. Elyx's SEO page names three of them (site size, competition and how much content is needed) and describes SEO as usually a monthly plan after an initial audit. What you cannot know without a quote is how competitive your own searches are, because that needs someone to look at them.

Affordable SEO Packages in India: What a Low Price Can Buy

An affordable package can be honest when its scope is clear, and LeadNexa's own page says the key is clear scope rather than the cheapest plan with vague or missing services. The arithmetic below shows why link totals are a poor yardstick.

  • LeadNexa Starter. ₹3,000 for 50 backlinks a month is ₹60 a link, if nothing else is counted.
  • WebHopers entry. ₹20,000 for 30 backlinks a month is about ₹667 a link, on the same assumption.
  • The gap. ₹667 divided by ₹60 is about 11, so the same word, "backlink", sits behind an 11-fold difference in price.

My reading: the figures do not prove one agency's links are poor, because each package bundles audits, reports and writing. They show that a link count alone tells you nothing about quality. Ask for a sample of last month's links and the pages they point to.

Local SEO Packages in India: When One Location Needs Less

A local SEO package suits a business with one to three physical locations, because its work centres on the Google Business Profile, citations and local pages. Savit's local table is built on exactly that, covering one, up to two or up to three locations, with profile setup and verification, citations and local schema (page markup that describes your business address). Savit's own page says a local package is usually the best fit under ₹20,000 a month, which is the agency's view, not an independent one.

Elyx's SEO page makes the same point for shops and clinics: your Google Business Profile, reviews and consistent address and phone details matter as much as the website. Choose a local package when customers search "near me". Choose a broader or e-commerce package when you sell to many cities or rank product pages.

Technical SEO and Content Marketing: Where the Hours Should Go

Technical SEO is front-loaded work and content marketing is the ongoing work, so a fair package shifts its hours from one to the other over the first months. Technical SEO means making sure search engines can find and read your pages: indexing, sitemaps, speed. Elyx's SEO page says the biggest wins are often signals that quietly contradict each other, and that technical fixes show up in weeks.

Content marketing means new pages and articles aimed at what customers search. LeadNexa lists one to eight articles a month across its plans, and Elyx's page describes proof-led articles tied to a service page. If a package repeats the same tasks every month for a year, ask what changes in month four.

The clearest red flags are a guaranteed ranking, a price before anyone has looked at your site, and results you cannot verify. None of the five top-ranking pages lists red flags, so this table is my reading, built from what those pages and the Elyx page say.

How Long Before an SEO Package Shows Results

Sources point to three to six months for noticeable results and longer for strong rankings, so treat any promise of days as a red flag. The three sources I have differ only at the edges.

SourceStated timeframe
PageTrafficA month or two to six or more; 3 to 6 months as a rule of thumb
LeadNexaTraffic in 3 to 5 months, stronger rankings in 6 to 12 months
Elyx SEO pageTechnical fixes in weeks; competitive local rankings in a few months

My reading: set a review at month three and judge it on indexing, impressions in Search Console and enquiries, not on a single ranking. Search Console is Google's own tool for seeing how your site performs, so you can check the agency's claims yourself.

A Worked Example: Costing a Year of SEO With GST and Term

Two packages that look ₹5,000 apart can differ by far more over a year once you add term and tax. This example uses each agency's own entry price from the table, not a forecast of what you will pay.

  1. WebHopers entry. ₹20,000 times 12 months is ₹2,40,000. GST at 18% is ₹43,200, so the year costs ₹2,83,200.
  2. Savit local entry. ₹15,000 times 6 months, the term in the heading of its local table, is ₹90,000. If 18% GST applies, which its page does not say, add ₹16,200 for ₹1,06,200.
  3. LeadNexa Growth. ₹5,000 times 12 months is ₹60,000, with GST not stated.

The three are not like for like. Compare them by cost per 12 months including GST, then by the deliverables you can verify.

Choosing a Package: A Decision Framework

Choose by what your business sells and where it sells, because that decides which package type fits. This framework is my reading of the criteria on the Savit and Elyx pages.

  • Choose a local package when you have one to three locations and customers search "near me", such as a clinic, salon or coaching centre.
  • Choose a national or e-commerce package when you sell to many cities or need product and category pages to rank.
  • Choose an audit first when the site has never been checked, so you know what is broken before you commit monthly.
  • Choose to fix the site first when it loads slowly or has no pages for your services, because SEO cannot rank pages that do not exist.

For who should do the work, freelancer vs agency vs small studio sets out the trade-offs. For the monthly care of the site itself, which is not SEO, see website maintenance charges in India.

What to Do Next

Ask three agencies the same questions and compare their written answers, rather than their price lists. Use this checklist:

  • [ ] I asked for the audit before the price.
  • [ ] I got the quote with and without GST.
  • [ ] I asked what changes in each month, not only month one.
  • [ ] I will own Search Console and GA4 access.
  • [ ] I asked how enquiries, not only rankings, are reported.
  • [ ] I know the notice period and what I keep if I leave.

Frequently asked questions

How much does SEO cost in India?

Published monthly SEO packages in India run from ₹3,000 to ₹1,50,000 or more, according to five agency pages read on 19 November 2026. LeadNexa's own page puts the range at ₹3,000 to ₹25,000+. The spread reflects scope, competition and locations, and every figure is the seller's own.

What is included in an SEO package?

A monthly SEO package should include an audit, technical fixes, on-page work, local profile work, content, analytics access and a monthly report. The five pages I read all list an audit, technical work and reporting. Ask for the number of pages and articles in writing.

Are cheap SEO packages worth it?

A cheap package is worth it only if its scope is clear and its deliverables are checkable. LeadNexa says real technical audits, genuine link building and transparent reporting matter more than the lowest price. Be wary of a low price paired with a very large link count.

How long does it take to see results from an SEO plan?

Most sources point to three to six months. LeadNexa cites 3 to 5 months for measurable traffic, and PageTraffic suggests giving SEO 3 to 6 months. Elyx says technical fixes show up in weeks and competitive local rankings take a few months.

Is GST charged on SEO packages?

Two of the five pages, PageTraffic and WebHopers, say 18% GST is added to their listed prices. The other three do not state it in the text I read. Ask for the quote with and without GST, and confirm tax treatment with a chartered accountant.

Do local SEO packages cost less than national ones?

Local plans are the entry tier in Savit's list, starting at ₹15,000 a month against a range that reaches ₹1,50,000+. Savit's page says local is usually the best fit under ₹20,000 a month. That is the agency's view, and the price depends on how many locations and keywords you target.

Can an SEO agency guarantee rankings?

No honest agency can guarantee a specific ranking. LeadNexa says Google's algorithm considers many factors outside an agency's control, and Elyx's SEO page says the same. Ask for a written commitment to work and reporting instead.

Should I sign a long SEO contract?

Prefer a short term with a clear exit. Terms in these sources range from month to month at PageTraffic, to no contract at SEO4LEADS and LeadNexa, to a six-month heading on Savit's local table. Ask the notice period and who keeps the content.

Which is the top SEO company in India?

This research found no independent ranking of SEO companies, so I cannot name one. Use the checks above: written deliverables, an audit before price, access in your name and reports on enquiries. Then compare three written proposals.

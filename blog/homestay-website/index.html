Homestay, Hotel and Resort Websites: Win Direct Bookings, Pay Less OTA Commission

A homestay website guide for India: OTA commission worked out, booking engines, UPI payments, Google listings and photos, with a checklist.

A homestay website wins direct bookings when a guest who found you on an OTA (online travel agency, such as MakeMyTrip or Booking.com) or on Google can check dates, see the price and pay a deposit on your own page, without a platform taking a cut. The qualification that matters is that a website does not fill rooms by itself: it converts guests you already attract, and it only saves money if your calendar stays in sync with every OTA you keep. The five pages we read for this article state no OTA commission rates, so the worked example below uses assumed numbers that you should replace with the rates in your own statements.

  • Commission. Only one page we read, a channel-manager vendor's, talks about commission, and its figures are marketing claims with no method shown.
  • Booking. Choose between a WhatsApp enquiry, a booking engine and a channel manager by how many rooms and how many OTAs you run.
  • UPI, Google and photos. No page we read documents UPI, Google hotel listings or photo practice, so those sections give you decisions and questions to ask, not borrowed rules.
  • Registration. Rules differ by state, and the only source we read on it is Kerala's tourism department.

Homestay Website vs OTA Listing: Where the Commission Goes

An OTA keeps a percentage of every booking it sends you, while a booking taken on your own homestay website costs you only the tools and payment fees you choose to pay for. The vendor page we read, Homeyhuts, lists losing commission on every OTA booking among the problems it sells against, and advertises zero commission on direct bookings. That zero describes the vendor's own cut, not your payment gateway's fee, so ask both what they charge.

Here is the arithmetic, using assumed inputs. A two-night stay at ₹3,200 a night is ₹6,400. At an assumed 15 percent commission, the OTA keeps ₹6,400 × 0.15 = ₹960. At an assumed 2 percent gateway fee on a direct booking, you pay ₹6,400 × 0.02 = ₹128. The saving is ₹960 − ₹128 = ₹832 per booking you move from an OTA to your own site.

Neither 15 percent nor 2 percent is a sourced rate. Read your own OTA statement and your gateway's fee page, then rerun the sum. To see when a site pays for itself, divide its written quote by your per-booking saving: a quote of N rupees needs N ÷ 832 moved bookings at these inputs.

Homestay Booking Websites: Engine, Channel Manager or WhatsApp

You can take direct bookings through a WhatsApp enquiry, a booking engine on your site, or a channel manager that also syncs your OTA listings, and the right pick depends on rooms and listings. The risk to design around is a double booking: the same night sold on two platforms. Homeyhuts' page frames its real-time sync as the fix and contrasts it with the delays of calendar-file (iCal) syncing.

This is my decision framework, built from that page and the studio's own facts.

Choose thisWhenWatch for
WhatsApp enquiry buttonOne to three rooms, one OTA at most, guests already message youDates and deposits tracked by hand
Booking engine on your siteYou want guests to see availability and pay a deposit themselvesIt does not talk to OTAs unless you add that

Custom-built bookingRules no tool handles, such as packages, seasons or group blocksHighest cost and the longest build

Homeyhuts also describes selling one property as single rooms, room combinations or the whole villa without conflicts, which is the case a resort with cottages will meet. Its page reports a 28 percent average revenue growth in the first 120 days, 2.5 times more direct booking revenue within the first year and 64 percent average occupancy. Those are one vendor's marketing figures with no method shown, so treat them as claims to test, not forecasts.

Homestay Website Template or Custom Design?

A template is enough for a single-room homestay with a WhatsApp button, and a custom build earns its cost once you need a calendar, payments and several room types. The template results we read on 16 November 2026 were mostly Framer templates and a WordPress theme with a homestay layout. A template gives you a look; it does not by itself give you availability, deposits or OTA sync, so check each of those separately.

The studio's draft ranges, from the site's price data, are ₹9,999–₹24,999 for a one-page site with an enquiry form and WhatsApp button, and ₹24,999–₹59,999 for a business site of up to ten pages with a CMS, Google Maps and SEO foundations. Typical timelines are 1–2 weeks and 2–4 weeks. These are indicative, never a quote, exclusive of 18% GST, and a fixed quote is confirmed in writing after a call.

Hotels and resorts differ from a homestay in structure, not in principle. They have more room types, seasonal rates, packages and group enquiries, so they sit higher on the booking table above. Online booking and UPI and card payments are separate options in the website cost calculator, so you can price a site with and without them.

Homestay Website Design: The Sections a Guest Needs

A homestay website design works when a guest can answer five questions in under a minute: what the rooms are, what they cost, where the place is, who the hosts are and how to book. The studio's industry pages for restaurants, clinics and salons share a pattern: a hero with one action, the offer, a gallery, trust details and a closing block with map, hours and WhatsApp. The order below adapts it and is my recommendation.

  1. Hero. One strong photo, the place name, the town, and a "Check dates" or "Book on WhatsApp" button.
  2. Rooms and tariff. Each room with a photo, sleeps-how-many, bed type and a price for a typical night.
  3. What is included. Meals, kitchen access, parking, Wi-Fi, and what costs extra.
  4. Location. A map, the road approach and the nearest town or station, in plain words.
  5. Host and reviews. A short note from the host and guest comments you have permission to show.
  6. House rules and cancellation. Check-in time, deposit and refund terms, written in advance.
  7. Book. Date picker or WhatsApp button, repeated at the bottom.

The text we extracted from Homestays of India includes template code that builds a WhatsApp message starting with "Hi" and the property's name, so each property page appears to open a pre-filled chat. That saves the guest typing and tells you which room they meant.

UPI Payments on a Homestay Website

UPI deposits suit Indian guests, but none of the five pages we read documents UPI, so confirm it with your booking tool or payment gateway before you promise it. MakeMyTrip says its payment gateway is secure, and Homeyhuts says its direct booking site accepts secure online payments and tracks payment status. Neither names UPI in the text we read.

What the studio states is narrower and first-hand: it can add UPI and card payments through a payment gateway on a build, and the calculator lists "UPI & card payments" as a separate option. A simple deposit flow looks like this, and it is my design, not a documented standard.

  1. The guest picks dates and the site holds them for a short, fixed time.
  2. The guest pays a deposit by UPI, and the booking is confirmed on screen.
  3. A WhatsApp message goes out with directions, check-in time and house rules.
  4. The balance is paid at check-in, and the refund terms you showed at step 1 apply.

If your tariff attracts GST, ask your CA what the invoice must show and who raises it. The salon booking article walks through the same UPI-deposit question for another trade, and the same caution applies: ask before you promise.

Google Hotel Listings and Google Maps: What to Check

Google hotel listings are the part of this topic where this article has no primary source, so it gives you questions to ask rather than rules. The research package contains no page from Google, so I have not verified how Google lists hotels or what it charges. Check Google's own Hotel Center and Business Profile help pages as of today.

What the studio does document is local search. Its SEO service covers setting up and optimising a Google Business Profile, its categories, photos and review process, and the studio's FAQ says your Profile and consistent address and phone details matter as much as the website for a place with a physical location. Use that, then put these questions to your booking vendor:

  • [ ] Can the engine or channel manager send my rates and availability to Google, and is that stated in writing?
  • [ ] Does my Business Profile show the same name, address, phone and website as my site?
  • [ ] Does the website link on the Profile open a page that loads fast on a phone?
  • [ ] Is the price a guest sees on my site no worse than on the OTAs, and have I decided my policy on that?
  • [ ] Are the photos on the Profile the same quality as the ones on my site?

The studio's free Google Business Profile checker is a place to start on the Profile side.

Registration and Legality: Check Your State Before You Advertise

Whether a homestay is legal to run and advertise depends on the state, and the only rule-making body we read on it is Kerala's tourism department. As of 16 November 2026 its page lists accredited hotels, resorts, homestays and heritage buildings, invites non-accredited providers to apply for classification, and lets accredited ones register once to publish a profile. It also links to accreditation guidelines.

The research contains no source for Karnataka or any other state, so I give no rule for them. Ask your state tourism department what registration or classification a homestay needs. This is a business-side note, not legal advice, and rules change, so confirm with the authority and a qualified professional.

If you hold a registration, show the number on your site so a guest can judge the place.

What to Do Next

Run your own commission sum first, then choose the lightest booking setup that stops double bookings. Copy this checklist before you pay anyone:

  • [ ] I know my real OTA commission and my gateway fee, and have rerun the arithmetic.
  • [ ] I counted my rooms and OTAs and picked a row from the booking table.
  • [ ] My vendor confirmed UPI, and Google feeds if I want them, in writing.
  • [ ] My Business Profile and site show the same name, address and phone.
  • [ ] I have a shot list and web-sized photos.
  • [ ] I checked registration with my state tourism department.

Frequently asked questions

How do I create a homestay website?

Start with a one-page site showing rooms, tariff, location, house rules and a WhatsApp button, then add online booking once you run more than a few rooms or OTAs. The section order above is a practical sequence.

Is a homestay website legit?

A homestay website is legit when its details can be checked outside the site: an address on Google Maps, a working phone number and a registration where your state requires one. Kerala Tourism, for example, publishes a list of accredited providers.

Is homestay legal in India?

Rules for running a homestay are set by each state, so there is no single answer, and this article gives none for Karnataka. Ask your state tourism department and a qualified professional before you advertise.

Can a homestay website really reduce OTA commission?

A homestay website reduces commission only on the bookings that move from an OTA to your own page, and only after you subtract your payment and software costs. At the assumed inputs above, each moved booking saves ₹832. Your own statements decide the true figure.

What is the best homestay website template?

There is no single best template, because a template is a design and the booking calendar, deposits and OTA sync are separate pieces. Pick one only after checking how bookings and payments will work.

Do I need a booking engine for a small homestay?

A homestay with one to three rooms and at most one OTA can start with a WhatsApp button and a manual calendar. A booking engine pays off when guests keep asking which dates are free, and a channel manager once you sit on two or more OTAs.

Can guests pay by UPI on my homestay website?

Yes, if your payment gateway or booking tool supports UPI, but none of the vendor pages we read states it. Confirm UPI in writing with the vendor before you put it on the page.

How do I get my homestay on Google?

Claim and complete your Google Business Profile first, with the same name, address, phone and website as your site. Whether your rates can also appear in Google's hotel results depends on your booking tool, so ask the vendor in writing.

How many photos does a homestay website need?

There is no sourced number, but cover each room, the food, the view, the host and the approach road, as in the shot list above. Compress every image, because the studio's care page warns that a 6 MB upload can slow a page.

How much does a homestay website cost in India?

The studio's draft range is ₹9,999–₹24,999 for a one-page site and ₹24,999–₹59,999 for a business site of up to ten pages, excluding 18% GST. Booking and UPI payments are options in the calculator. These are indicative, never a quote, and a fixed quote is confirmed in writing after a call.

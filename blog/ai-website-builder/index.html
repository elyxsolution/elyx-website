Can AI Build Your Website? What AI Website Builders Can and Can't Do

An AI website builder can draft a site in minutes. See what five vendor pages claim, where a draft breaks for Indian businesses, and a test to run first.

Yes, an AI website builder can produce a working first draft of your website in minutes: pages, text, images and a mobile layout from a short description. What it does not do is finish the job, because the facts, the payments, the languages, the domain and the ownership are still yours to check. The honest qualification is that I did not run these tools myself: this page rests on five vendors' own pages read on 20 November 2026, and gives you a test to run on your own business.

  • Can do. Draft a multi-page site, write the page copy, generate images, fit phones and publish in one click, by the vendors' own account.
  • Cannot be assumed. Correct facts, Indian payments and GST invoices, local languages and an easy way out, none of which the five pages' text mentions.
  • Free is a starting point. Custom domains, online payments and extra features sit on paid plans on the pages that say.
  • Next step. Run the 60-minute test below before you pay anyone, a builder or a studio.

What Is an AI Website Builder and How Does It Work?

An AI website builder is a service that turns a written description of your business into a ready-made site, then lets you edit it by typing requests or by dragging parts around. Wix describes the flow as describe, watch it build, edit with its assistant or drag and drop, then connect a domain and publish. Base44 says one sentence about the site's purpose and feel is enough to start.

Underneath, the tool picks a layout, writes text, creates images and wires in a template for forms or a shop. Squarespace calls the result a "multi-page starting point". My reading is that this phrase is the most accurate description on any of the five pages.

What AI Website Builders Can Do Well

AI builders are strongest at the blank-page work: choosing a structure, drafting text and filling a layout that works on a phone. The five pages agree on this list.

  • Structure. Squarespace says it generates a full multi-page site shaped around what you offer and what visitors should do.
  • Text and images. Base44 and Replit say they write copy and create images that fit it, so you are not left with dummy text to replace.
  • Phones. Wix, Design.com, Base44 and Replit each say the site adapts to phone, tablet and desktop automatically.
  • Hosting and publishing. Wix claims 99.99% uptime; Design.com and Replit offer one-click publishing; Base44 says hosting, SSL and a domain are handled.
  • Editing in plain language. Replit lets you click an element and describe the change, and Wix pairs prompting with drag and drop.

For a landing page or a simple brochure site, my reading is that this covers a real share of what a small owner needs. The rest of this page is about what it does not cover.

A 60-Minute Test You Can Run on Your Own Business

The fairest test of an AI website builder is your own business, run on a free plan with a written brief and a clock. The times below are my suggested split, not a measured result.

  1. Write the brief (10 minutes). Use five lines: what you sell, to whom, the one action you want, your three most-asked questions and your languages. The website brief template has the fields.
  2. Generate on two builders (10 minutes). Paste the same brief into two tools so you compare output, not marketing.
  3. Fact-check the text (10 minutes). Mark every number, timing, address and claim. Any sentence you cannot verify from your own records comes out.
  4. Test on your phone (10 minutes). Open it on your own phone over mobile data, then try to take the one action in two taps.
  5. Test the Indian parts (10 minutes). Add a WhatsApp click-to-chat link, try a UPI payment if the plan allows one, and see whether an invoice can carry a GSTIN.
  6. Find the exit (10 minutes). Read the export and domain terms, and note the price of the plan that removes the limits you hit.

Score each step pass or fail. My rule of thumb is that three or more fails mean the draft is a sketch, not a site.

Which AI Website Builder Is Best?

No source I read ranks these tools against each other with a stated method, so no honest "best" exists on this evidence. Design.com calls itself "the #1 best website builder" and Wix lists its own criteria, which is how each vendor wins its own test.

Wix's criteria are design quality, ease of use, scalability and added features. My reading adds three that an Indian owner should weigh more: the export path, the Indian payment and invoice options, and the price after the trial. Choose by those, on your own brief, not by a vendor's headline.

When to Use an AI Builder and When You Need a Professional

Choose an AI builder when the site is small, the facts are few and a mistake is cheap to fix, and choose a professional when the site must take money, hold data or last. This decision rule is my analysis, built from the failure points above.

  • Choose an AI builder for a one-page profile, an event page or a trial of an idea, where you can check every line and nobody pays through the site.
  • Choose a template builder with hand edits for a small brochure site when you want control of layout; Wix, WordPress or a custom site compares them for Indian small businesses.
  • Choose a professional when you need payments, bookings, logins, several languages, a clean search setup or a site that must grow without a rebuild.

In a rule-of-thumb sense, the more the site must do after the visitor arrives, the less the draft helps. The tool saves the cheap part of the work and leaves the costly part.

Will AI Replace Website Builders and Web Developers?

On this evidence, AI is changing who does the first draft, not removing the need for someone accountable for the finished site. Wix itself describes the product as a hybrid of AI prompting and hands-on editing, and Squarespace calls its output a starting point.

Anything that needs a decision about money, data or law still needs a person who answers for it. The same applies to AI inside a site: what AI agents are and where they need a human shows why an assistant that acts without a check is a risk, and a chatbot that answers from your own approved content is a safer shape.

Frequently asked questions

Can AI help me build a website?

Yes, AI can draft the structure, text, images and mobile layout of a website from a short description. You still need to check every fact, connect your domain and decide how payments and enquiries work. All five vendor pages I read describe the output as something you then edit.

Do AI website builders work?

They work for producing a first draft quickly, and the evidence for more than that is thin. The vendors' speed claims range from seconds to under an hour, and none of the five pages gives data on how often the draft is published unchanged.

Are AI website builders good for a business site?

They are good for a simple site you can check line by line, and weaker where the site must take payments, bookings or logins. For an Indian business, the five pages do not mention UPI, GST invoices, WhatsApp or Indian languages, so test those yourself.

Is an AI website builder free?

You can start free on all five pages, but a custom domain, online payments and extra features sit on paid plans where the pages say so. Wix states this directly, and Base44 lists more credits and a free domain for a year among its paid upgrades.

Is the Wix AI website builder free?

You can start building for free on Wix, but connecting a custom domain or collecting payments online needs a Premium Plan, per Wix's own page on 20 November 2026. Wix also offers a free domain for a year with an annual plan. Check its pricing page for current plan prices.

Which AI website builder is best?

No source I read compares them with a stated method, so there is no evidence-based winner. Pick by your needs: how easily you can export, whether Indian payments and invoices work, and what you pay after the trial.

Can I own and move an AI-built website?

Whether you can move it depends on the vendor's terms, and none of the five pages I read explains export. Read the export, domain and content terms before you build, and keep your own copy of the text and images.

Will AI replace web developers?

AI is taking over the blank-page draft, but not the accountability for payments, data and the long-term site. Wix and Squarespace both describe their output as something you refine by hand, which supports that view.

Will an AI-built website rank on Google?

An AI-built site can be indexed like any other, but no vendor page promises a ranking and none should. Wix lists SEO tools, and its claim of a 15% traffic increase for users rests on an unnamed Google study that I could not check.

When should I hire a professional instead?

Hire a professional when the site must take payments, run bookings, hold customer data, work in several languages or grow past pages and forms. If your 60-minute test shows three or more fails, that is the signal.

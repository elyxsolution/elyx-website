Wix vs WordPress vs a Custom Website for Indian Small Businesses

Wix vs WordPress India, plus a custom site: a three-year cost in rupees from sources that disagree, where builders hit limits and when to switch.

Wix vs WordPress India comes down to this: Wix is the faster start for a small brochure site you will edit yourself, self-hosted WordPress is cheaper to run over three years and easier to leave, and a custom website suits a business that has outgrown both. The qualification that matters is price, because the two sources I read that price Wix for India put its plans roughly six times apart. The table below shows both and tells you how to settle it at Wix's own checkout.

  • Cost. My arithmetic gives ₹10,000 to ₹22,000 over three years for self-hosted WordPress on one source's rates, against ₹11,000 to ₹21,000 for Wix on the same source, but a dollar-based source puts Wix Core near ₹1,01,800.
  • Limits. Three sources agree that a Wix site cannot be moved to another host, so leaving means a rebuild.
  • Effort. WordPress is cheap only while someone updates it; one source puts a properly maintained site at ₹40,000 to ₹80,000 a year.
  • Switch. Move when you need a growing store, a content plan, bookings or hundreds of pages, not because the site looks dated.

Wix vs WordPress India: Three Routes and Six Criteria

A fair Wix vs WordPress India comparison fixes its criteria first: three-year cost, ownership and exit, search and speed, payments and GST, effort to run, and room to grow. Wix is a hosted builder, where one company supplies the editor, hosting and security. WordPress here means the free WordPress.org software on hosting you rent. A custom website is code written to your brief and owned by you.

There is a naming trap. WordPress.com is a hosted service that works more like Wix, and Wix's own comparison page and the All About Cookies review both compare Wix with WordPress.com. So no, Wix is not a WordPress site: the two are separate products, and this article compares Wix with the self-hosted kind.

Every source I read sells something. Wix sells plans, HostKarle sells hosting, NeerSoft and BtechWaleTech sell builds, and Elyx Solutions sells custom builds. I name the source beside each figure, and I read all of them on 17 November 2026.

Wix vs WordPress Pricing in India: Total Cost Over 3 Years

Over three years the cheapest self-hosted WordPress route costs less than the cheapest Wix route in the two sources that price both, but the ranges overlap and one source prices Wix several times higher. As of October 2026, Daily Web Digest lists Wix Light at ₹250 a month and Core at ₹500 a month, excluding 18% GST. On 17 October 2026, HostKarle recorded Wix's public page showing US$17.77 a month for Light and US$29.77 for Core, and HostKarle notes that Wix's page says the final price varies by location and appears at checkout.

The two sets differ by a wide margin. My arithmetic, at HostKarle's indicative rate of 95 rupees to the dollar: US$17.77 comes to about 1,688 rupees a month against the 250 on Daily Web Digest's list, about 6.8 times. For Core, US$29.77 comes to about 2,828 rupees against 500, about 5.7 times. I cannot tell which a reader in India will be charged, and HostKarle sells hosting, so treat the checkout page as the tiebreaker.

Where Wix Hits Its Limits, and Where WordPress Does Too

The limit the sources agree on most is exit: a Wix site cannot be moved to another host, so outgrowing it means a rebuild. NeerSoft says Wix has no meaningful export. Daily Web Digest says Wix requires the site to stay on its servers and lets you export product lists but not the design, and HostKarle says you can move the domain but switching systems is a rebuilding project.

Wix's own pages describe the reverse move, importing a WordPress blog's posts into Wix, with pages moved separately. The other limits named:

  • Page count. Tooltester says the Wix editor slows down on sites with many pages.
  • Phones. Tooltester says Wix keeps a separate mobile layout you may need to rearrange, so check every page in mobile view before publishing, as BtechWaleTech advises.
  • Stores. Tooltester says Wix store navigation allows one sub-level and multi-currency selling is limited. NeerSoft calls it fine for a handful of products.
  • Web address. All About Cookies notes the free Wix plan gives a random-looking address, and Tooltester says a custom domain needs a paid plan.

WordPress has limits too, and they are about people. Daily Web Digest points to WordPress's own hardening guide, which puts updating core, themes and plugins near the top. Tooltester warns that free themes from untrusted sources can carry malware, and that when something breaks you contact your host or a developer.

Where WordPress is stronger is ownership, exports and choice: Wix counts over 800 apps in its App Market against over 50,000 plugins in WordPress's directory, and W3Techs reported in September 2026, via BtechWaleTech, that WordPress runs 40.2% of all websites. Where Wix is stronger is effort. NeerSoft, a WordPress shop, says a local business with three to eight pages and no store is better served by Wix than by an ₹80,000 agency build.

SEO and Speed: Does the Platform Decide Your Rankings?

Both platforms can rank, and the sources differ only on the ceiling: Wix covers titles, descriptions, alt text, sitemaps, redirects and schema options in its settings, while WordPress adds the same through plugins such as Yoast or Rank Math. Daily Web Digest says no platform will rank a site, and that useful content, a mobile-friendly site and a complete Google Business Profile matter more than the builder. WordPress pulls ahead on custom URL structure, schema and hundreds of location pages, per Daily Web Digest and BtechWaleTech.

Speed is where sources conflict. Wix cites HTTP Archive's Core Web Vitals report for August 2026: 87% of Wix sites passed all three measures on desktop against 53% of WordPress sites. Tooltester's study of 400 sites found Wix pages loaded slower than other builders, with a bigger gap on mobile.

My reading: a vendor's pass rate and a reviewer's study measure different things, so neither settles your site. A WordPress page's speed depends on its theme, plugins and hosting, as BtechWaleTech notes. Test your own page on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data; Google's good thresholds, as BtechWaleTech quotes web.dev, are a largest paint within 2.5 seconds, interaction under 200 milliseconds and layout shift of 0.1 or less.

An AI builder changes how the first draft is made, not how Google ranks it. All About Cookies found Wix's AI-written copy lacked depth, and that platform does not explain a site with no customers. If yours has none, start with why a cheap website is not getting customers.

Payments, UPI and GST Invoices on Each Platform

All three routes can take UPI through an Indian gateway, but none gives you a finished GST invoice setup out of the box. As of October 2026, Daily Web Digest says Wix needs the Core plan or higher to accept payments. HostKarle lists Razorpay, PayU India, Cashfree, Paytm, Easebuzz, airpay and Nimbbl on Wix's India list, and says to confirm the current options in your Wix dashboard.

On WordPress, WooCommerce adds the store, and Daily Web Digest says Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU and PhonePe publish free plugins in the WordPress.org directory. WooCommerce also supports cash on delivery. A custom site connects the same gateways through their documentation, with the checkout built to your flow.

For GST invoices, Daily Web Digest says Wix has India-specific apps in its App Market, one with a free tier of 20 invoices, and WooCommerce has free and paid extensions whose last update date you should check. Each gateway charges its own fee on every platform, so ask for its rate card by payment method. I did not read the GST rules themselves, so confirm invoice requirements with your CA. For larger catalogues see e-commerce website cost in India.

A Custom Website: What It Adds and When Another Option Wins

A custom website adds ownership, speed tuning and a fit to your own workflow, and it costs the most up front, so for a five-page brochure site it is often more than you need. That range includes a CMS for editing, Google Maps, speed and SEO foundations. Hosting, domain and any care plan, indicatively ₹9,999–₹24,999 a month, are separate lines.

Custom wins when you need booking logic, logins, a portal or a checkout shaped to your business, which plugins stretch badly. Wix wins when you will edit weekly yourself, have no budget for a build and expect a small site for years. WordPress wins when you want ownership at a low cost and have someone to maintain it. When to Switch From Wix, and How to Keep Your Rankings

Switch when the site has to do something Wix cannot, or when staying costs more than leaving. NeerSoft names the triggers: ecommerce beyond a few products, content and SEO as a real strategy, memberships or bookings, and a design that outgrew the editor. A dated look alone is not a trigger.

Follow these steps, in order:

  1. Secure the domain. Keep registration and admin logins in an account you control, as HostKarle advises.
  2. Export what exports. Take product lists and blog posts per Daily Web Digest, and copy page text and images by hand.
  3. List the pages that bring visitors. Use Search Console and your Google Business Profile as the check.
  4. Build on staging. Keep the same URLs where you can, and set a 301 redirect for each one that changes.
  5. Launch, then watch. Check Search Console for errors in the weeks after.

We audit what a site has, keep what earns traffic and rebuild the rest, and a business website is indicatively 2–4 weeks. That is a draft planning figure, not a delivery date.

What To Do Next

Spend an hour on four checks before you pay anyone. Write down your page count, whether you sell online, who will edit and what you expect in three years. Open Wix's India checkout for the plan that takes payments and note the billing period, renewal price and GST. Price WordPress hosting at its renewal rate, and name who will update it.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, Wix or WordPress, for a small business in India?

Wix is better if you will build and edit the site yourself and want nothing to maintain, and WordPress is better if you want ownership, a lower long-run cost and room to grow. For three to eight pages with no store, NeerSoft calls Wix the right answer and Daily Web Digest calls it the easiest to design with. Add a content plan or a larger store and both point to WordPress.

Is WordPress better than Wix?

WordPress is better on ownership, exit and scale, and Wix is better on ease and upkeep. Neither is better at ranking, since Daily Web Digest says content, mobile speed and a complete Google Business Profile matter more than the platform. Which is better depends on who maintains the site.

Is Wix a WordPress site?

No, Wix is a separate hosted builder and not built on WordPress. WordPress.org is free software you install on your own hosting, and WordPress.com is a hosted service built on it. Wix's pages compare Wix with WordPress.com, so check which one a review prices.

Is WordPress cheaper than Wix in India?

On the rates I read, a self-hosted WordPress site you maintain yourself costs less over three years: ₹10,000 to ₹22,000 against ₹11,000 to ₹21,000 for Wix on the rupee list. Sources disagree on Wix's price, with one dollar-based figure near ₹1,01,800 for Core. Pay a developer to maintain WordPress and the total rises sharply.

Can I move my site from Wix to WordPress later?

You can move the domain, but the site itself has to be rebuilt. NeerSoft says Wix has no meaningful export, and Daily Web Digest says product lists export while the design must be recreated. Plan redirects so the URLs that bring visitors keep working.

Is Wix bad for SEO?

No, Wix handles titles, descriptions, alt text, sitemaps and redirects, and NeerSoft calls the reputation for poor ranking outdated at the scale Wix suits. The ceiling is lower for hundreds of pages, custom schema or fine speed control. Tooltester's 400-site study found Wix pages loaded slower, so test your own.

Can Wix take UPI payments in India?

Yes, on the Core plan or higher through an Indian gateway. HostKarle lists Razorpay, PayU India, Cashfree, Paytm, Easebuzz, airpay and Nimbbl on Wix's India list as of October 2026. Confirm the current options and your merchant eligibility in your Wix dashboard.

Is Wix or WordPress easier for a beginner?

Wix is easier for a beginner, because the editor, hosting and templates are ready at sign-up. Tooltester and BtechWaleTech both say WordPress needs more setup and more choices. WordPress gets easier after a developer builds it, when staff only add posts or change prices.

The right payment gateway for a website in India is the one whose written quote gives the lowest total cost for your payment mix, pays out on a schedule you can plan around, and signs up your type of business. As of November 2026, Razorpay lists 2% plus 18% GST, Cashfree advertised 1.6% to new merchants for up to 12 months, and PayU lists 2% or 3% by payment method. The honest qualification is that most sources here are the gateways' own pages, and none of them documents Stripe, so this page cannot rate it.

  • Fees. Compare the whole bill (rate, GST, exclusions, the rate after an offer ends), not the headline percentage.
  • UPI. Zero bank charges on UPI do not mean the gateway's own fee is zero.
  • Settlement. Paytm and Cashfree publish options; for the rest, get the schedule in writing.
  • Sign-up. GST and a live website or app matter for some gateways and not for others.

Read the sources with their interests in mind. Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, Paytm, PhonePe and Pine Labs wrote the pages about themselves, and Toucan Pay is a gateway too. Wise sells a competing product for receiving foreign money, so I give its figures as its reading of each gateway's pricing.

Worked Example: What 2%, 1.6% and a Mixed Payment Bill Cost

On ₹5,00,000 of successful sales in a month, a 2% rate costs ₹11,800 including GST and a 1.6% rate costs ₹9,440. The inputs are illustrative: the percentages come from the table and GST is 18% on the fee.

The 1.6% case saves ₹2,360 a month, or ₹28,320 over 12 months, but only while the introductory rate lasts. Razorpay's guide tells buyers to calculate the cost after any offer ends. The mixed case costs ₹1,180 more than a flat 2% (₹12,980 minus ₹11,800); PayU's table does not list UPI, so this is a method-mix illustration, not a UPI quote.

A single ₹5,000 order at 2% costs ₹100 plus ₹18 GST, which is ₹118. Razorpay's guide says to compare providers on the same payment mix and period, and to track recoverable tax separately. A chartered accountant can tell you whether GST on gateway fees is recoverable for you.

Free Payment Gateway for Website in India: What "Free" and "0%" Cover

A free payment gateway in India usually means no setup fee or annual maintenance charge, not no transaction cost. Wise lists PhonePe's Standard Plan as free, Razorpay and Paytm state no setup or annual maintenance fee, and Instamojo has a Lite plan at ₹0 a year. None removes the per-sale fee once any introductory period ends.

Razorpay's guide spells out why a "0% offer" is not free. GST still applies during the offer, and the guide says all 0% offers exclude some methods (American Express, EMI and international payments among them). Razorpay says its own offer excludes five categories, so check the duration, the volume cap and the rate afterwards.

Search results also show third-party tools claiming zero commission. I did not read their terms, so I do not rate them.

UPI Payment Gateway for Website in India: Fees, Methods and Autopay

Every gateway here accepts UPI, but accepting it is not the same as charging nothing for it. Wise reports over 21 billion UPI transactions in India in January 2026, so a gateway must handle the major UPI apps and peak-hour volume.

Razorpay's guide separates two charges: standard bank-to-bank UPI carries zero bank MDR (the fee a bank charges a merchant), while Razorpay charges its own 2% platform fee plus GST unless your offer or contract says otherwise. Pine Labs advertises 0% MDR on UPI with a 10-minute go-live, limited to merchants with GST and a website or app. These claims may not describe the same thing, so ask each provider for the rate per payment method, in writing.

  • Flows. Cashfree lists static and dynamic QR and intent flows, with 8+ direct bank integrations for UPI.
  • Recurring. PhonePe names AutoPay and Paytm names UPI Autopay. Razorpay's guide says to evaluate its Subscriptions product, and its charges, separately.

Onboarding Documents, and Payment Gateway for Individuals in India

The sources do not publish a full document list, so the dependable answer is what each page states and what you must ask. Pine Labs limits its offer to merchants with GST and a mobile app or website. Paytm promises digital onboarding with minimal documentation for eligible businesses, and says its monthly limit depends on line of business, documents submitted and website or app readiness.

Cashfree's FAQ asks for business details and scanned business documents, and in the text I could read it also tells some applicants to share personal address proof instead of business registration details. The extract does not say which applicants, so ask before assuming it covers you. Cashfree supports NRO accounts but not NRE accounts, and says you need no website to start, because Payment Links and Payment Forms work without one.

A May 2026 thread on r/developersIndia asks how to find a gateway for an unregistered business, so the gap is real. My reading is that individuals should look first at payment links (Cashfree, Razorpay) and at Instamojo, which Wise describes as selling without a custom website. No source documents any gateway's rules for unregistered sellers, so each sign-up form is the only authority.

Where Stripe Fits, and What This Page Cannot Say About It

This page cannot rate Stripe, because no source in its research documents Stripe's India fees, availability, UPI support, settlement or sign-up rules. The title names Stripe because the content plan asked for it, and I would rather say so than guess. Stripe's own India pages are where to check whether your business type is accepted.

The sources do say who else covers overseas buyers. Cashfree lists international cards in 140+ currencies, at 2.69% per Wise, PayU lists 3% for international transactions, and Paytm says international payments need no additional APIs. Wise notes that gateways are built mainly for domestic payments, and that overseas clients bring slow remittances and unclear exchange costs.

My rule, not sourced: if most buyers pay in rupees, compare the Indian gateways first. If most pay in foreign currency, ask Stripe and at least one Indian gateway for written quotes on the same payment mix.

Connecting a Gateway to Your Website: Plugin, Payment Link or Custom Checkout

There are three ways to connect a gateway: a payment link needs no site changes, a plugin suits Shopify, WooCommerce or Wix, and a custom checkout calls the gateway's API. A custom build also receives webhooks, which are automatic notifications of each payment event, so your site can mark orders paid.

Razorpay, Cashfree and Pine Labs each list developer documentation and a way to test, and Razorpay names webhooks. Ask your developer to test a failed payment, a refund and a payment that stays pending, because Wise says failed and pending UPI payments are common.

The account should be yours. Who Owns Your Website? says the gateway account and its KYC belong in your business's name, with settlements going to your bank, so a change of developer never touches your money.

What Usually Goes Wrong With Gateway Choices

Most gateway problems come from comparing the wrong number or meeting a condition after sign-up. The causes come from the sources, and the fixes are my reading.

  • The cheapest rate costs more. Cause: headline rates compared. Fix: same mix, GST, fixed charges and exclusions for every quote.
  • A "free" offer ends. Cause: duration, cap and exclusions missed. Fix: model month 13 and the excluded methods.
  • UPI treated as free. Cause: zero bank MDR confused with the gateway fee. Fix: ask for the rate per method.
  • Money arrives late. Cause: settlement cycle never asked. Fix: get the cycle, holiday rule and any faster-payout fee in writing.
  • Sign-up is delayed. Cause: GST or site readiness not checked. Fix: read eligibility first (Pine Labs, Paytm).
  • Foreign cards cost more. Cause: international rate overlooked. Fix: check it (Cashfree 2.69%, PayU 3%).
  • The developer holds the account. Cause: opened in their name. Fix: open it in your business's name.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a payment gateway for my website in India?

You apply on the gateway's website, submit its business and bank details, wait for approval, then connect it by plugin, payment link or API. Paytm and Cashfree describe digital onboarding, and requirements differ by provider. Check GST and website rules before applying.

Which payment gateway is best for an ecommerce website in India?

No source here names one best gateway, because the answer depends on payment mix, platform and settlement needs. Toucan Pay's guide says the same, citing volume, order value, customer location and payment methods. The decision table above matches gateways to business types.

What are the payment gateways in India?

The ones named in the sources are Razorpay, Cashfree, PayU, PhonePe, Paytm, CCAvenue, Pine Labs and Instamojo. PhonePe's article lists six of them, and Wise lists five for small businesses. Stripe is not covered by this research.

Is there a free payment gateway for a website in India?

Not in the sense of zero cost per sale. PhonePe's Standard Plan is listed as free, and Razorpay and Paytm charge no setup or annual maintenance fee, but a per-transaction fee plus GST applies once any offer ends.

Does UPI cost anything to accept on a website?

Usually yes, through the gateway's own fee, even though bank MDR on standard UPI is zero. Razorpay says its 2% platform fee plus GST applies unless your offer or contract says otherwise. Pine Labs advertises 0% MDR on UPI for eligible merchants, so ask in writing.

Can I get a payment gateway without a registered business?

It depends on the gateway, and the sources do not settle it. Pine Labs requires GST, while Cashfree's page mentions personal address proof in place of business registration details and offers links without a website. Confirm on the sign-up form.

What is the difference between a payment gateway and a payment aggregator?

A gateway passes payment details securely between customer, merchant and bank, while an aggregator offers many payment methods through one platform and can handle wider acceptance and settlement. Toucan Pay and Cashfree both draw this distinction. Ask which one your provider is.

How long do payments take to reach my bank account?

It depends on the gateway and plan: Paytm lists T+1, same-day and on-demand, and Cashfree lists 15 minutes, end of day or on demand. Wise says timelines vary with banks and holidays. Get the cycle and any faster-payout fee in writing.

Can I use Stripe for an Indian website?

This research could not confirm it, because no source documents Stripe's India availability, fees or onboarding. Check Stripe's own India pages for your business type. Then compare its written quote with an Indian gateway on the same payment mix.

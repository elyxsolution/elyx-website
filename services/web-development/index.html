Service 01 · Web Development

Web development that turns visitors into enquiries.

Elyx builds fast, mobile-first websites and web apps for Indian businesses. A focused landing page starts at ₹9,999 and ships in 1–2 weeks; larger sites, web apps and e-commerce stores take 4–8 weeks. You get a fixed quote before any work begins.

Who this is for: Clinic owners, coaching centres, shops and founders who need a site that brings in enquiries, not just a brochure.

Most small-business websites fail in the same three places: they load slowly on a phone, they bury the one action a visitor should take, and nobody on the owner's side can update them without calling a developer. We build against those three problems first, then worry about polish.

Every project is done by the three founders, so the person you brief on the first call is the person who designs the page and writes the code. That removes the usual agency hand-offs, and it is why a landing page can realistically ship in one to two weeks.

What you get

What you get with a web build

01

Mobile-first design

Most of your visitors arrive on a phone, often on a patchy 4G connection. We design for that screen first and scale up, not the other way around.

02

Speed you can measure

We tune for Core Web Vitals: compressed images, lean code and sensible hosting, so pages feel instant and Google has one less reason to rank you lower.

03

Enquiry paths that work

Contact form, WhatsApp click-to-chat, call button and Google Maps, wired up and tracked so you can see which one brings the most leads.

04

A CMS you will enjoy using

Where it makes sense we add a headless CMS so your team can change prices, photos and text without touching code. We also record a short walkthrough.

05

SEO foundations built in

Clean URLs, structured data, sitemap, meta tags and local-search basics are part of the build, not a paid extra after launch.

06

E-commerce when you need it

Catalogue, cart, UPI and card payments, order emails and an admin panel, built the same way as everything else: fast, simple and yours to own.

Process

How a web project runs

Our usual flow is a short discovery call, a scoped proposal, design, a build with two rounds of feedback, QA on staging, then launch. You are looped in at every stage.

  1. 1

    Discovery call Day 1–2

    A short call to understand your business, your customers and the one action that makes the site worth it.

  2. 2

    Scoped proposal Day 2–3

    A written scope with timeline and a fixed price. No surprise line items mid-project.

  3. 3

    Design Week 1

    Wireframes first, then the visual design. You approve the direction before a line of code is written.

  4. 4

    Build and feedback Week 1–3

    We build in weekly increments with two rounds of feedback, so you see real, working pages as they come together.

  5. 5

    QA, staging and launch Final week

    Testing across devices, a staging review with you, then launch, handover and 30 days of post-launch support on the Launch tier.

Questions

Common questions, honest answers.

How much does a website cost?

Engagements start at ₹9,999 for a landing page or redesign. A multi-page business site, an online store or a custom web app is scoped after a discovery call and quoted in writing before work begins. Our pricing page and cost calculator show typical ranges.

How long does it take to build a website?

A landing page or small marketing site usually ships in 1–2 weeks. E-commerce and web apps take 4–8 weeks. The timeline is agreed before we start and we flag blockers early.

Can I update the site myself after launch?

Yes. We build for handoff: where it makes sense we integrate a CMS, document everything and run a walkthrough so routine edits never need us.

I already have a website. Can you redesign it without hurting my Google rankings?

Yes, redesigns and migrations are a big part of our work. We audit what you have, keep what earns traffic (URLs, content, SEO equity) and rebuild the rest properly. Ashirwad Dental Clinic is a good example.

What technology do you use?

We choose what fits the job: React, Next.js and TypeScript for most front ends, Node.js, FastAPI or Python on the back end, PostgreSQL or MongoDB for data. We avoid proprietary lock-in that would force you to depend on us.

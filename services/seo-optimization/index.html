Service 04 · SEO Optimization

SEO that is judged by enquiries, not traffic.

Elyx fixes the technical foundations search engines penalise, builds the local and content signals they reward, and reports on enquiries rather than visits. SEO engagements start at ₹9,999 and begin with an audit of what is holding your site back.

Who this is for: Small businesses and founders who want customers to find them on Google, in Maps and increasingly inside AI answers.

Search has changed. Google's AI Overviews now answer many generic how-to questions on the results page, which means traffic from broad articles is falling for everyone. What still brings customers is being the obvious answer to a buying search: "dentist near me", "website developer in Bengaluru", "coaching centre for IELTS".

Our SEO work therefore starts with the money pages and local signals, then adds content that only a real business with real experience can write. We measure it in enquiries, and we tell you when something is not working.

What you get

What our SEO work covers

01

Technical SEO audit

Canonicals, redirects, sitemaps, indexing, structured data and page speed. Often the biggest wins are fixing signals that are quietly contradicting each other.

02

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Setting up and optimising your profile, categories, photos, reviews process and local schema, which is where most small businesses are actually found.

03

Service and industry pages

One page per service and per customer type, each written to rank for a specific buying search and to send visitors to a clear next step.

04

Content with first-hand proof

Articles built around your own prices, projects and checklists. This is the kind of content that still earns clicks and gets cited in AI answers.

05

Core Web Vitals and speed

Faster pages rank better and convert better. We fix what is slowing yours down.

06

Analytics and reporting

Search Console and GA4 set up properly, with events for calls, WhatsApp clicks and form submissions, and a monthly report on which pages actually produce enquiries.

Process

How an SEO engagement runs

Our usual flow is a short discovery call, a scoped proposal, design, a build with two rounds of feedback, QA on staging, then launch. You are looped in at every stage.

  1. 1

    Audit Week 1

    We crawl the site, check indexing and local presence, and list what is wrong in order of impact.

  2. 2

    Fix the foundations Week 1–3

    Technical fixes, redirects, sitemap and structured data, so everything you publish afterwards can actually rank.

  3. 3

    Build the money pages Week 2–6

    Service, industry and location pages, plus your Google Business Profile.

  4. 4

    Publish proof-led content Month 2 onward

    A steady rhythm of articles tied to a service page, scored on business potential, proof and durability before we write them.

  5. 5

    Measure and adjust Monthly

    Monthly report from Search Console, GA4 and your enquiry log. We double down on what produces leads and drop what does not.

Questions

Common questions, honest answers.

How long does SEO take to work?

Technical fixes can lift indexing within weeks. Ranking for competitive searches takes months, and anyone promising page-one rankings in days is guessing. We agree what to measure and review it monthly.

Do you guarantee rankings?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. We guarantee the work: a clean technical foundation, strong service pages, honest reporting and an enquiry-based view of results.

Is local SEO different from normal SEO?

Yes. For businesses with a physical location, your Google Business Profile, reviews and consistent address and phone details matter as much as your website. We set up both together.

Will AI search replace SEO?

Google says AI Overviews use the same index and ranking as normal search, so strong pages with original, first-hand content help in both. We focus on content that cannot be replaced by a summary: real prices, real timelines, real results.

How much does SEO cost?

Engagements start at ₹9,999, and ongoing SEO is typically a monthly plan scoped after an audit. See pricing for typical ranges.

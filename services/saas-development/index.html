Service 02 · SaaS Development

SaaS development, from first customer to ten-thousandth.

Elyx designs and builds multi-tenant SaaS products end to end: data model, authentication, roles, subscription billing, admin and analytics dashboards. An MVP typically takes 8–16 weeks, scoped and quoted up front, and engagements start at ₹9,999 for a scoped discovery.

Who this is for: Founders and operators who already have the problem and a paying-customer idea, and need a team that can ship version one.

A SaaS product is not a website with a login. The decisions you make in the first two weeks, how tenants are separated, how permissions work, how billing states are modelled, are the ones that are expensive to change later. We spend real time on them before we write screens.

We are a team of three, and all three of us work on a SaaS build. You get architecture, interface and growth thinking in the same room, without a project manager translating between them.

What you get

What we build into a SaaS product

01

Architecture and data modelling

A schema and service layout designed around how your product will grow: tenants, workspaces, usage and the reports you will want in a year.

02

Authentication, roles and permissions

Sign-up, login, invitations, team roles and the permission model that keeps one customer's data away from another's.

03

Subscriptions and billing

Plans, trials, upgrades, invoices and failed-payment handling, integrated with a payment gateway that supports Indian cards, UPI and international customers.

04

Admin and analytics dashboards

The internal view you need to run the product: who signed up, who is active, who is about to churn, and a customer-facing dashboard people actually enjoy.

05

Turning a manual process into software

If your business runs on spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups or a person's memory, we map the workflow and turn it into a product your team or your customers can use.

06

Production-ready engineering

Monitoring, backups, environments and a clean deployment path from day one, so launch week is not the first time anyone checks if it can be restored.

Process

How a SaaS project runs

Our usual flow is a short discovery call, a scoped proposal, design, a build with two rounds of feedback, QA on staging, then launch. You are looped in at every stage.

  1. 1

    Discovery and scoping Week 1

    We map the users, the workflow and the one metric that decides if version one worked. Output: a written scope and a cut line for what ships first.

  2. 2

    Architecture and design Week 1–3

    Data model, permissions, wireframes and the core screens. You sign off on how it works before we build it.

  3. 3

    Build in weekly increments Week 3–10

    Working software every week, with two structured feedback rounds. You use the product as it comes together, never a black box.

  4. 4

    QA, staging and launch Final 2 weeks

    Testing, staging review with real data, billing in test mode, then production launch and handover.

  5. 5

    Care and iteration Ongoing

    After launch we monitor, fix and keep shipping against your roadmap, on a maintenance plan if you want one.

Questions

Common questions, honest answers.

How much does it cost to build a SaaS product in India?

It depends on scope. Engagements start at ₹9,999 and a focused MVP is usually scoped after a discovery call. We price by outcome rather than by the hour, and the quote is in writing before work begins. See pricing for typical ranges.

Do I need a full product or can we start with an MVP?

Start with the smallest version that proves the idea. We help you draw a cut line: what must ship in version one, and what waits until real customers ask for it.

Who owns the code?

You do. We build on standard, widely used technology and document everything so you are never locked in to us.

Can you take over or rescue an existing product?

Yes. We start with a short audit of the code, hosting and data model, tell you honestly what is worth keeping, and then fix or rebuild accordingly. This is common with prototypes built quickly with AI tools that now need real engineering.

How do payments and contracts work?

50% upfront to begin and 50% on final delivery, covered by a straightforward statement of work with scope, deliverables, timeline and revision rounds. No retainer lock-ins unless you want one.

An AI assistant that answers your calls, so no caller is missed.

Elyx builds AI voice assistants that pick up your business calls, understand what the caller needs, book a slot or take a message, and pass urgent calls to your team. Engagements start from ₹9,999, quoted to your requirements.

Who this is for: Clinics, salons, service businesses and sales teams that miss calls when everyone is busy and lose customers to the next number on the list.

A missed call is often a lost customer, and most small teams cannot answer every call at every hour. A voice assistant can answer straight away, ask the right questions, book a slot or take a message, and send you a summary.

Voice is less forgiving than chat, so we start with one narrow job, such as answering inbound calls and booking appointments, and test it with practice calls before it talks to a customer. Outbound calling is a different matter: telecom rules apply, so we confirm what is allowed before building it.

What you get

What an AI call assistant includes

01

Answers inbound calls

The assistant picks up, greets callers in a tone that suits your business and works out what they are asking for.

02

Qualifies and books

It asks the questions your team would ask, then books an appointment or records the details for a call back.

03

Hands over to a person

For urgent, upset or complicated callers it transfers the call or flags it to your team straight away.

04

Call summaries

After each call you get a short summary and the caller's details by WhatsApp, email or in your CRM, so nothing depends on memory.

05

Your callers' languages

Tuned and tested for the languages and accents your callers use, with scripts from your own business, before launch.

06

Recording and consent

Call recording and notices are set up the way your advisor confirms they should be, and outbound calling is only built after the rules are checked.

Process

How a voice assistant project runs

Our usual flow is a short discovery call, a scoped proposal, design, a build with two rounds of feedback, QA on staging, then launch. You are looped in at every stage.

  1. 1

    Discovery call Day 1–2

    We learn how calls reach you today, what callers ask and what a good answer sounds like.

  2. 2

    Scoped proposal Day 2–3

    A written scope covering the call flows, handover rules, integrations and languages, with a quote tailored to you.

  3. 3

    Write the call flows Before we build

    We script the conversations, including the awkward ones, and agree what the assistant must never say.

  4. 4

    Build and test calls Weekly

    We build the assistant and run practice calls, then tighten it until you are happy to put it live.

  5. 5

    Launch and review After launch

    We start with a small share of calls, review the early ones with you and fix what needs fixing.

Questions

Common questions, honest answers.

Will callers know it is an AI?

We recommend telling callers they are speaking to an automated assistant, and we build that in by default. It builds trust and avoids confusion. How you handle it is your decision and we advise you on it.

Can it handle Hindi, Kannada and other languages?

Often yes, but quality varies by language, accent and phone line. We test with realistic examples from your callers before launch and tell you honestly where it struggles.

What if it cannot help a caller?

It says so, takes the caller's details and flags the call to your team, or transfers it straight away if that is set up. It should never leave a caller stuck.

Can it make outbound calls?

It can be built to, but outbound calls are covered by telecom rules, including TRAI's regulations on commercial calls and messages. We start with inbound calls, and we only build outbound calling after we have confirmed with you what is permitted for your use.

Shopify vs WooCommerce India comes down to who looks after the store and how much you sell each month. For a first-time Indian seller with no developer on call, Shopify is the safer start, because it hosts, patches and secures the store for a plan fee. WooCommerce turns cheaper once monthly sales are high enough that Shopify's extra fee on every third-party gateway sale costs more than paying someone to maintain it. The qualification is that every source I read is an agency or a platform vendor, and none gives an independent test or the GST rule itself.

- **Fees.** Shopify Payments does not operate in India, so each Shopify sale pays Shopify's fee and the gateway's. WooCommerce charges only the gateway.
- **The crossover.** With upkeep at ₹10,000 a month, Shopify Basic costs more than WooCommerce above about ₹4.65 lakh in monthly sales (my sums, shown below).
- **GST.** Neither platform makes a GST-compliant invoice on its own, so budget an app or plugin on both.
- **Verdict.** Shopify for most small stores and heavy cash-on-delivery sellers. WooCommerce for WordPress and content-led shops, and sellers with a developer.

## Shopify vs WooCommerce India: The Criteria and the Short Answer

Shopify rents you a finished, hosted store, while WooCommerce is a free plugin that turns a WordPress site you host into a store, so the choice is mostly about who carries the upkeep. I compare them on six criteria: fees in rupees, GST invoicing, payments and cash on delivery, shipping tools, upkeep and speed, and fit by store size. Facts are as read on 17 November 2026, and prices change, so check Shopify's own [India pricing page](https://www.shopify.com/in/pricing) before you budget.

| Criterion | Shopify | WooCommerce |
|---|---|---|
| Platform cost | Plan from ₹1,499 a month, billed yearly | Plugin free; hosting, extensions, upkeep paid |
| Fee on each sale | Extra fee on third-party gateways, 2% on Basic | None from the platform; gateway fee only |
| Hosting and security | Shopify's job | Yours, your host's or a developer's |
| GST invoice | Needs an app or service | Needs a plugin |
| Cash on delivery tools | Many apps built for Shopify first | Fewer plugins; full database access |
| Razorpay features | Payment gateway only | Gateway, Route and Subscriptions |
| Mobile speed | 76% of stores pass Core Web Vitals | 35% pass |
| Fastest launch (one agency) | 14 days | 28 days |

The rows come from the sources in the sections below, and the launch times are one agency's own fastest builds, not a benchmark.

## What Each Platform Bills You, in Rupees

Shopify bills a monthly plan plus an extra fee on each sale, while WooCommerce bills for hosting, paid extensions and upkeep instead of a plan. The table gives Shopify's India plans as ExactWhy read them on Shopify's pricing page on 9 October 2026, with the Advanced rates from DevIt International and the DEV Community post.

| Plan | Billed yearly (₹ a month) | Billed monthly (₹ a month) | Extra fee, third-party gateway |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | 1,499 | 1,994 | 2% |
| Grow | 5,599 | 7,447 | 1% |
| Advanced | 22,680 | 30,164 | 0.6% |

The extra fee exists because Shopify Payments is not available in India, according to DevIt International and the DEV Community post, which both point to Shopify's supported-countries list. Every Indian store therefore uses a third-party gateway such as Razorpay, PayU or Cashfree and pays the gateway's percentage on top. WooCommerce's own payments product is also unavailable in India, per DevIt.

The sources disagree on the fee. The DEV Community post says Shopify no longer prints the percentages on its India page, so get the current rate from Shopify before you commit. Dynamic Dreamz, whose plan prices differ from the India page as the others read it, says the fee disappears from its ₹6,600 plan upward, while ExactWhy and DevIt show 1% on Grow. WooCommerce's own post lists 1%, 0.6% and 0.2% for Grow, Advanced and Plus on US plans, but it is a vendor comparing itself to a rival.

WooCommerce's lines are hosting at ₹800 to ₹3,500 a month (Innovatrix Infotech), or ₹300 to ₹800 on shared hosting (Dynamic Dreamz), plus paid extensions and upkeep. DevIt reports price guides putting upkeep at ₹10,000 to ₹40,000 a month in India if you pay someone. Build prices are in the studio's [e-commerce cost guide](/blog/ecommerce-website-cost-india/).

## Worked Example: Where Shopify's Per-Sale Fee Overtakes WooCommerce Upkeep

Shopify Basic starts costing more than a maintained WooCommerce store at roughly ₹4.65 lakh in monthly sales if upkeep is ₹10,000 a month, and the figure moves a long way with that one input. The arithmetic: Shopify Basic on yearly billing is ₹1,499 × 12 = ₹17,988 a year, plus 2% of twelve months' sales, so 17,988 + 0.24 × monthly sales.

| Monthly sales (₹ lakh) | Shopify Basic, a year (₹) |
|---|---|
| 1 | 41,988 |
| 3 | 89,988 |
| 5 | 1,37,988 |
| 10 | 2,57,988 |

The ₹3 lakh row matches DevIt's own worked example. WooCommerce's running cost is the other side of the sum. The crossover is its yearly cost minus 17,988, divided by 0.24.

| WooCommerce yearly cost (₹) | How it is built | Shopify Basic costs more above |
|---|---|---|
| 9,600 | Hosting ₹800 a month, you do the upkeep | Never, before your time |
| About 50,000 | What the DEV Community post's ₹13 lakh a year switch point implies | ₹1.33 lakh a month |
| 1,29,600 | Hosting ₹800 plus upkeep ₹10,000 a month | ₹4.65 lakh a month |
| 5,22,000 | Hosting ₹3,500 plus upkeep ₹40,000 a month | ₹21 lakh a month |

The second row is my back-calculation. At ₹13 lakh a year, 2% is ₹26,000, and with ₹23,928 for a monthly-billed Basic plan the total is about ₹50,000. That post gives no upkeep figure, so I cannot confirm what it assumed.

The sums leave out the gateway fee (the same on both), themes, apps, extensions, build cost and GST. Upgrading from Basic to Grow pays for itself at about ₹4.10 lakh a month on yearly billing, or ₹5.45 lakh on monthly billing, which agrees with Innovatrix's "past ₹5 lakh". My reading: upkeep, not the plan price, decides the crossover.

One open question: no source says whether Shopify's fee applies to cash-on-delivery orders, which do not pass through a gateway. If 40% to 60% of your orders are COD, as Innovatrix says for many Indian D2C brands, ask Shopify before you apply 2% to all sales.

## GST Invoicing: Neither Platform Does It Out of the Box

Neither Shopify nor WooCommerce produces a GST-compliant invoice on its own, so both need an invoicing app or plugin. ExactWhy, reading Shopify's Help Centre India GST page on 9 October 2026, says Shopify can charge CGST and SGST within your state and IGST on interstate sales once you set the rates. The same page says Shopify does not create a GST-compliant bill and points merchants to a local invoicing service or an App Store app.

HSN codes, the goods classification codes on GST invoices, go in Shopify's HS tariff code field, which needs at least six digits. On WooCommerce you enter tax rate tables yourself and add a plugin for invoices, then keep it compatible with every update. The work is similar, and the difference is who maintains the tool afterwards.

The research on the top-ranking pages found none that answers GST invoicing, so this is the gap. My checklist for testing an invoicing app or plugin, not a legal list:

- **Tax split.** Does it show CGST and SGST for in-state orders and IGST for other states?
- **HSN.** Does each line carry its code?
- **Returns.** Does a return produce a credit note?
- **COD.** Do cash-on-delivery orders get an invoice too?
- **Buyer GSTIN.** Can a business buyer enter a GSTIN at checkout?

What an invoice must legally show, your rates and your registration thresholds come from the GST authorities and your chartered accountant. The research package contained no GST rule text, so I state none, and I did not check the rules on 17 November 2026. Rules change, so confirm with a qualified professional.

## Payments and Cash on Delivery

Both platforms take UPI, cards and net banking in India through the same third-party gateways, so the difference lies in the extras. Razorpay's plugin table, as ExactWhy read it on 9 October 2026, ticks Payment Gateway, Route and Subscriptions for WooCommerce and only Payment Gateway for Shopify. Route splits a payment between vendors. If you plan marketplace payouts or Razorpay subscriptions, check how you would do it on Shopify before committing.

Razorpay's own fee is the same on both platforms. The DEV Community post reads its pricing page as 2% on domestic payments, with a 0% promotion for the first 90 days. Promotions end, so read Razorpay's [pricing page](https://razorpay.com/pricing/) yourself.

Cash on delivery is where the tools differ most. ExactWhy says COD verification, partial prepayment and return-to-origin (RTO) prediction apps are built for Shopify first, because most Indian D2C brands sell there, and WooCommerce has fewer choices and more testing. Innovatrix argues the opposite for a team with a developer: WooCommerce's database access lets you build OTP confirmation, WhatsApp pre-delivery messages and a block on high-RTO pincodes without paying an app. My reading: Shopify is faster to a working COD flow, and WooCommerce is cheaper to bend if someone can code it.

## Shipping and Courier Integrations

Neither platform ships parcels. Both connect to a courier or aggregator through an app or plugin, and the sources I read say very little about which connections exist. Innovatrix's fastest WooCommerce build, with a clean theme, Razorpay, a Shiprocket connection and basic SEO, took 28 days against 14 for Shopify. ExactWhy lists courier integration in its Shopify setup and says COD, courier and WhatsApp tools matter from the first month.

No source gives a courier's plugin list, rate card or sync behaviour, so I name no working pairing. Ask the courier or aggregator these before you choose a platform:

1. Is there an official app or plugin for your platform, and for your version?
2. Does it push order, weight and address data automatically, or by file upload?
3. Does tracking flow back to the order and reach the buyer by WhatsApp or email?
4. Does it handle COD remittance and RTO status?
5. Who supports it when a platform update breaks it?

Question five is where the platforms split. On Shopify the app developer fixes it. On WooCommerce, WooCommerce's support policy, quoted by ExactWhy, excludes general WordPress support, third-party plugins and customisations.

## Speed, Security and SEO

Shopify stores pass Google's mobile speed checks more often, but WooCommerce is the more common platform. DevIt International cites the 2025 Web Almanac: 76% of Shopify stores had good Core Web Vitals on mobile against 35% of WooCommerce stores, and WooCommerce ran on 44.4% of ecommerce sites on mobile against Shopify's 25.3%. DevIt adds that the gap is about setup, not a WooCommerce limit.

Security follows the same split. DevIt cites Patchstack counting 11,334 new WordPress-ecosystem vulnerabilities in 2025, 91% in plugins, so a WooCommerce store needs weekly updates, backups and a staging copy.

On SEO the sources disagree, and I label this practitioner opinion. Dynamic Dreamz says WooCommerce wins on URL control, Yoast and Rank Math plugins and blogging, and notes Shopify's fixed /products/ and /collections/ prefixes. DevIt says WooCommerce is not automatically better and that a well-structured store with good content can rank on either. My reading: platform matters for content-led brands, and little for a catalogue-first seller.

## Verdict by Store Size

For most Indian stores under about ₹4 lakh a month in sales and without a developer, Shopify is the better start, and above about ₹5 lakh with maintenance in place WooCommerce starts to win on fees. The table sets the sources' advice beside my sums.

| Situation | Lean toward | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Under about ₹2 lakh a year online | Neither yet | DEV Community post: sell by Instagram and WhatsApp with a payment link |
| First store, no developer, to about ₹4 lakh a month | Shopify | Upkeep outweighs the fee at this size |
| COD above half of orders, no developer | Shopify | Ready-made COD and RTO apps (ExactWhy) |
| Launch deadline of a few weeks | Shopify | 14 days against 28 in Innovatrix's builds |
| Developer or plan in place, ₹5 lakh a month or more | WooCommerce | Fee exceeds upkeep in the example above |
| Content-led brand or existing WordPress site | WooCommerce | Keeps the content, the editor and the SEO work |
| Razorpay Route or Subscriptions are central | WooCommerce | Razorpay's plugin table |
| Unusual pricing or checkout logic | WooCommerce or custom | Full code access (Innovatrix) |

Each side has a real case. WooCommerce.com argues that Shopify's closed code means your roadmap follows theirs, and cites Shopify's 2023 retirement of its old checkout customisation system, which forced merchants to rebuild. That is a vendor's claim, but it names a real risk of renting. Shopify's strengths are the ones Innovatrix and ExactWhy name: no servers, a curated app store and a faster first launch.

## What Usually Goes Wrong

Most bad platform choices come from pricing the visible plan and skipping the lines around it.

| What goes wrong | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Budget built on the headline plan | The per-sale fee and apps sit outside it | Add 2% of sales on Basic and price your apps |
| Wrong Shopify price quoted | Pricing page shows the yearly rate first (DEV Community) | Check ₹1,499 against ₹1,994 for your billing term |
| "Free" WooCommerce with no owner | Nobody named for updates and backups | Name a person or a plan before launch |
| GST invoicing left to last | Neither platform includes it | Choose and test the app or plugin first |
| COD treated like prepaid | RTO and fake orders erase margin | Add COD verification and model the loss |
| Update breaks the store before a sale | Plugin conflicts, no staging copy | Test on staging, update weeks before a festival |

## FAQ

### Which is better, Shopify or WooCommerce, for an Indian seller?

Neither wins for every seller: Shopify suits most first stores without a developer, and WooCommerce suits content-led shops and sellers with someone to maintain it. Past about ₹4.65 lakh a month in sales, with upkeep at ₹10,000 a month, WooCommerce's fees come out lower.

### Why do people say Shopify is better than WooCommerce?

Because Shopify handles hosting, security and updates, launches faster and has more ready-made Indian COD apps. DevIt adds that 76% of Shopify stores pass mobile Core Web Vitals against 35% for WooCommerce, a gap it blames on setup.

### Is Shopify available in India?

Yes, Shopify sells plans in India, but Shopify Payments does not operate here. Every Indian store must use a third-party gateway such as Razorpay or Cashfree.

### Does Shopify charge a transaction fee in India?

Yes, on third-party gateway orders, and ExactWhy read the rates on 9 October 2026 as 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow and 0.6% on Advanced. The DEV Community post says Shopify no longer prints them on the India page, so confirm with Shopify.

### Which is cheaper for an Indian store?

It depends on sales and upkeep. In my sums Shopify Basic is cheaper below roughly ₹4.65 lakh a month if WooCommerce upkeep is ₹10,000 a month, and WooCommerce is cheaper above it.

### Does Shopify or WooCommerce create GST invoices?

Neither does on its own. Shopify's Help Centre, as ExactWhy reads it, says Shopify does not create a GST-compliant bill, and WooCommerce needs a plugin. Confirm what your invoice must show with your accountant.

### Which handles cash on delivery better?

Shopify has more ready-made COD verification and RTO apps, according to ExactWhy. WooCommerce can be built to do the same without an app fee, according to Innovatrix, but it needs a developer.

### Is WooCommerce better for SEO than Shopify?

Not automatically. Dynamic Dreamz favours WooCommerce for URL control and blogging, while DevIt says a well-built store with good content can rank on either.

### Can I start on WooCommerce and move to Shopify later?

Yes. DevIt says products, customers and orders can be exported and imported, but themes and apps do not carry over, so plan permanent redirects from every old URL to keep search rankings.

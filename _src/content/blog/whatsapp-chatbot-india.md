## What a WhatsApp Chatbot Is, and How an AI One Differs

A WhatsApp chatbot is software that reads customer messages on a business number and replies without a person typing each answer. IT Marketz describes two kinds: a flow bot that answers from fixed menus, and an AI bot that uses a model such as GPT-4o or Claude to read free text.
My reading is that most small Indian businesses should run a hybrid. Buttons and forms handle bookings and orders, where a wrong answer costs money, and an AI layer answers free-text questions only from content you have approved. The wider difference between a bot and an agent is covered in [What Are AI Agents? A Plain-English Guide for Business Owners](/blog/what-are-ai-agents/).

## WhatsApp Business API: Why a Chatbot Needs the Platform

A chatbot that replies on its own needs the WhatsApp Business Platform, usually called the API, because the free WhatsApp Business app is built for a person replying by hand. Brainguru lists what the app gives you (a profile, catalogue, labels, quick replies, greeting and away messages) and what only the platform gives you: automated flows, template broadcasts to large opted-in lists, CRM connections and a shared inbox for several agents.

There are two routes to the platform, per Brainguru. Most businesses go through a Business Solution Provider (BSP), such as Wati, AiSensy, Interakt or Gupshup, and a business with its own developers can connect to Meta's Cloud API directly. Nexta adds that a number already on the Business app can usually be moved, fully or in coexistence mode where Meta offers it, so ask what happens to your existing chats.

The website end is the easy part. Elyx Solutions' [web development service](/services/web-development/) lists a WhatsApp click-to-chat button, call button and Google Maps among the enquiry paths it wires up and tracks, and the bot itself runs on the platform behind that button.

## How to Set Up a WhatsApp Chatbot, Step by Step

Setting up a WhatsApp chatbot takes five steps, and the slowest ones wait on Meta rather than on the developer. The sequence and weeks follow Nexta's published plan.

1. **Pick the questions (week 1).** Read a real chat export, choose the questions that repeat most and draw where a person takes over.
2. **Verify and connect (weeks 1 to 2).** Meta Business verification, the WhatsApp Business Account, display name approval, the phone number and the first templates.
3. **Build the flows (weeks 2 to 3).** Buttons, lists and forms, plus links to your store, CRM or payment gateway.
4. **Test it hard (week 3).** Your team tries to break it, and native speakers check Hindi or Kannada wording.
5. **Launch and read transcripts (week 4).** Read the first fortnight of chats and fix where people drop off.

Quoted timelines differ with what is built. Botsense says 48 to 72 hours on its own platform, subject to Meta verification, Nexta says 2 to 4 weeks for a custom bot, and IT Marketz says 2 to 3 weeks for a basic flow bot and 3 to 5 for an AI bot.

Brainguru warns that verification and template approval can take longer than building a simple flow. Ask any vendor for a week-by-week plan that marks which steps wait on Meta and which on you.

## The Pricing Model: Four Layers and Meta's Published Rates

A WhatsApp chatbot's price is four costs stacked: setup, a platform or provider fee, Meta's message charges and custom development. Brainguru, which also publishes a WhatsApp provider called LeadNXT, declines to list market ranges because vendor figures change often and rarely say what is included. A fifth line appears if the bot uses a language model, because IT Marketz lists model usage as an extra billed by the model provider.

Meta's layer is the one with public numbers. As of 1 October 2026, per Brainguru's reading of Meta's INR rate card, a delivered message costs:

| Category | Used for | Rate per delivered message |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing | Offers and announcements | ₹0.8631 |
| Utility | Order updates, appointment reminders | ₹0.115 |
| Authentication | One-time passwords | ₹0.115 |
| Service | Free-form replies inside the 24-hour window | ₹0.115 after 1,000 free a month per number |

These are list rates before tax and any provider markup, and messages customers send you are free. Here is a made-up month to show the arithmetic:

| Message mix | Sum | Meta charge |
|---|---|---|
| 600 reminders sent outside a chat window | 600 × ₹0.115 | ₹69.00 |
| 2,000 promotional messages | 2,000 × ₹0.8631 | ₹1,726.20 |
| 1,400 service replies on one number | (1,400 − 1,000) × ₹0.115 | ₹46.00 |
| **Total** | | **₹1,841.20** |

Brainguru's own sum scales the point: 50,000 marketing messages at ₹0.8631 come to ₹43,155. My reading is that a bot built for support and reminders has a small Meta bill, so its plan fee and any markup matter more, while a bot built for promotion is driven by the message bill.

The sources disagree in three places, so check Meta's current rate card before you budget:

- **Service replies.** Nexta says replies inside 24 hours are not charged, and Brainguru says a free tier of 1,000 applies from 1 October 2026.
- **Ad window.** Brainguru says messages after a click-to-WhatsApp ad can be free for up to 7 days, while the [WhatsApp booking guide](/blog/whatsapp-booking-for-website/) cites WhatsApp's pricing page at 72 hours on 17 November 2026.
- **Old model.** IT Marketz and WACTO still describe per-conversation charges, while Nexta and Brainguru say billing became per message in July 2025.

## Rules: Consent, Templates, Human Hand-Off and Data Protection

WhatsApp's rules, as the sources describe them, come down to consent, approved templates, a path to a person and a number you own. This is a business-side summary read on 20 November 2026, not legal advice, and rules change.

- **Consent.** Nexta says Meta's policy expects people to have agreed to hear from you before you message first, so keep a record of each opt-in.
- **Templates.** Messages you start use templates that Meta reviews, and Brainguru says aggressive promotional copy is often rejected.
- **Real platform only.** Nexta says WhatsApp Web scrapers and bulk-sender apps break WhatsApp's terms and can get a number banned along with its history.
- **A person on call.** Nexta says Meta's policy expects a clear path to a human.
- **Data protection.** Brainguru says the main duties under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 apply from 13 May 2027.

I did not read the Gazette notification, so confirm the date and your duties with a qualified adviser.

## What Usually Goes Wrong With WhatsApp Chatbots

Most failures come from setup choices, not the AI: the number, the hand-off, the data link and the bill. The table is built from Nexta's and Brainguru's pages.

| Mistake | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Number banned | A bulk tool or WhatsApp Web app | Use the official platform on a number you own |
| Customers trapped in menus | No route to a person | Add a "talk to someone" button and a shared inbox |
| Bot cannot see orders | Not connected to your data | Read your store or ERP through an API |
| Launch slips | Verification rounds, template rejections | Start verification first and budget copy time |
| Surprise bill | Marketing volume or provider markup | Model your message mix and ask for list-rate pass-through |
| Wrong answers | The bot guesses | Answer from approved content and read early transcripts |
| Lock-in | Accounts held in the vendor's name | Hold the Meta assets yourself and ask about export |

## Which Route to Choose and What to Do Next

Choose the lightest route that covers your message volume and your systems. These rules are my analysis from the tables above, not sourced findings.

- **A few chats a day:** use the free Business app with quick replies.
- **A handful of flows or campaigns:** take a self-serve provider plan and test with its trial.
- **The bot must read orders, fees or slots, or take payments:** commission a custom build on the platform.
- **Enterprise volume, many languages or voice:** look at the enterprise providers.

Before you sign with anyone, run this checklist:

1. List your ten most common customer questions from real chats.
2. Decide what the bot must never answer and where it hands over.
3. Ask for item-by-item quotes covering setup, plan, Meta charges and development.
4. Ask whose name the WhatsApp and Meta accounts will be in.
5. Ask for a working demo on your own questions, and test the hand-off.
6. Pilot with one use case before you add others.

## FAQ

### What is a WhatsApp chatbot?

A WhatsApp chatbot is software that replies to customer messages on a business WhatsApp number without a person typing each answer. It may follow fixed menus or use an AI model to read free text. Either kind should pass the chat to a person when it cannot help.

### Is a WhatsApp chatbot free?

Parts of it are free, but a custom automated bot is not. The Business app and Meta's agent cost nothing to start, and a free tier such as AiSensy's lets you test flows. To make one for free, start there; platform, provider and build fees apply once the bot reads your own systems.

### How do I create a WhatsApp chatbot for my business?

Pick your repeat questions, verify your business with Meta, connect a number to the Business Platform, build and test the flows, then launch and read transcripts. Use a provider's no-code builder or commission a custom build.

### Do I need the WhatsApp Business API for a chatbot?

Yes, for a bot that replies on its own. Brainguru says automated flows, large template broadcasts, CRM links and shared inboxes need the platform, directly or through a provider. The free app suits a person replying by hand.

### How much does a WhatsApp chatbot cost in India?

It depends on four layers, and no single price is reliable. Nexta lists builds from ₹24,999 plus GST and IT Marketz lists an AI bot from ₹80,000, both excluding Meta's charges. Plan fees start near ₹1,500 a month on AiSensy's listing.

### How much does Meta charge per WhatsApp message in India?

Brainguru reports a rate card effective 1 October 2026 of ₹0.8631 per delivered marketing message and ₹0.115 for utility, authentication and service messages. Each number gets 1,000 free service messages a month. Check Meta's own rate card, because sources disagree on some details.

### How do I add a WhatsApp chatbot to my website?

Put a click-to-chat link or button on your page that opens your business number, and run the bot on the Business Platform behind it. The website holds only the link.

### Can a WhatsApp chatbot reply in Hindi, Kannada and other languages?

Often yes, but quality varies. Botsense lists Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Bengali, and Nexta covers Bengali, Hindi and English. Test with your customers' real messages and have native speakers check the wording.

### Is a WhatsApp chatbot allowed in India?

Yes, within WhatsApp's rules. Sources say you need consent before you message first, approved templates for messages you start and a path to a person, and bulk-sender tools risk a ban.

One freelance developer's page, read on 19 November 2026, puts a custom coaching app at ₹2 to 8 lakh upfront plus ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month, against ₹18,000 to ₹50,000 a year for ready-made platforms. ## What a Coaching Institute App Does That a School App Does Not

A coaching institute app tracks a student's progress toward an exam, while a school app mostly reports attendance and daily updates to parents. The developer page I read describes a coaching app as one place for batches, tests, doubts and results, with test schedules, mock results and doubt queues built for exams such as JEE and NEET. It says school apps lean toward parent updates, sports and arts.

A feature list copied from a school app therefore misses the test and doubt-solving loop that coaching centres sell. Searches for "coaching app" also return personal and fitness coaching products, which this article does not cover.

## Booking, Reminders and Fee Payments: The Three Jobs in the Title

Booking, reminders and fee payments are the three jobs that touch every student and parent, so they are the best place to start. Each has a clear way to run on a phone in India.

**Booking.** Elyx's booking page describes live availability, confirmations, reminders and optional deposits, and it lists coaching centres as one audience. For a coaching institute the booked thing is usually a demo class, and class capacity matters more than staff calendars.

**Reminders.** Reminders by WhatsApp go through the WhatsApp Business Platform, where, per Elyx's WhatsApp automation page, business-started messages need the customer's consent and approved templates. The developer page adds a practical point: WhatsApp-native tools need no app download from parents, which avoids app fatigue when families juggle school, tuition, sports and music classes. The [options for WhatsApp booking](/blog/whatsapp-booking-for-website/) are compared in a separate article.

**Fee payments.** Elyx's payments page describes UPI, card and net banking through a gateway such as Razorpay, Cashfree or PayU, with the gateway, not your app, handling card details. It also describes confirming payment from the gateway's own signals rather than the customer's browser. Here is a fee flow assembled from those pages, as my sketch and not a measured result:

1. **Store the schedule.** Each batch has a fee amount and due dates.
2. **Send a reminder.** An approved WhatsApp template goes out before the due date.
3. **Collect.** The parent pays by UPI or card through a link or an in-app screen.
4. **Confirm.** The gateway confirms the payment to your system.
5. **Receipt and ledger.** A receipt is issued and the admin screen marks the fee paid.

Gateway account verification can take time outside any developer's control, so start it early. If the answer is an app, [Elyx's app development service](/services/app-development/) covers payments, push notifications and store submission.

## Website for Coaching Institute: Often the First Step, Not the Last

A website for a coaching institute is usually the cheaper first step, because it carries course pages, fees, a demo-class form and a WhatsApp button without anyone installing anything. Elyx's coaching centres page lists a page per course with batch timings, a short demo-class form, plainly shown fees and tracking of form, WhatsApp and call events.

The same page notes that Indian regulators have issued guidelines on misleading coaching advertisements, including claims about selection and rank. An app listing is advertising too, so every result you show needs proof you can produce.

The developer page says a progressive web app (a website that behaves like an installed app) or a no-code tool is enough for 90% of coaching use cases. That is an opinion with no method behind it, so do not treat it as data. Elyx's app page gives a sharper test: an app earns its cost when you need push notifications, offline use, device features or daily repeat use, which [Does Your Business Need an App, or Just a Better Website?](/blog/does-my-business-need-an-app/) works through.

## Coaching Institute Management Software: Ready-Made Tools First

Ready-made coaching institute management software covers attendance, tests, messaging, study material and fee tracking, and for a standard institute it is the quickest and cheapest route. The developer page names Teachmint, Classplus and FeeAlert, and says tasks such as messaging parents, sharing material, collecting fees and tracking attendance work well on such platforms. It says they get you started in days, not months.

The same page claims FeeAlert is free for up to 20 students and needs no app download from parents, and describes Classplus's fee as non-refundable and paid upfront. I have not checked any vendor's own page, so read each current plan and refund rule before you pay.

Choose a ready-made tool when your workflows are standard and you want results this month. Choose something else when you have tried one and found a gap you can name.

## White-Label vs Custom: Choosing Between Five Routes

A white-label app is a pre-built app that you brand as your own, and it costs less than a custom app but more than a no-code build, according to the developer page. That page gives no rupee figure for it. The table collects every figure the page does give, as of 19 November 2026, and each is the page's claim.

| Route | Cost the page states | Time the page states | Control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ready-made (Teachmint, Classplus) | Teachmint ₹18,000 to ₹36,000+ a year; Classplus ₹25,000 to ₹50,000+ a year | Days | Their features, their rules |
| No-code (Glide, Adalo) | $50 to $300 a month in platform fees | 4 to 12 weeks | Limited by the platform; you do not fully own the app |
| White-label | No figure given | 4 to 8 weeks including store review | Your brand, their features |
| Web app (PWA) studio | No figure given | As little as one day once logo, colours and content are supplied | Less control than custom |
| Custom | ₹2 to 8 lakh upfront plus ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month | 6 to 9 months | Full, if the contract assigns you the code |

The page is inconsistent on one row: ₹18,000 to ₹36,000+ for Teachmint in one place and ₹18,000 to ₹50,000 for "platforms like Teachmint" in another. I read the second as a range across platforms. Elyx's published timeline for a scoped mobile app is {{weeks:mobile}}, shorter than 6 to 9 months, so build time depends on what is scoped.

**Choose white-label when** the features are standard and you want your own name on the app stores. **Choose custom when** you need something nobody sells, such as offline access, AI doubt solving or links to systems you already run, which are the examples the developer page gives. With white-label, ask who holds the store accounts and the student data, and whether you can leave with both.

## What Coaching Institute App Development Costs: A Three-Year Worked Example

Over three years, a custom app costs 7.4 to 17 lakh rupees on the developer page's own figures, while a ready-made plan costs between ₹54,000 and ₹150,000. These are another party's numbers, and they do not include Elyx's. The table shows the arithmetic.

Any app quote moves with the number of screens, a custom back end, payments, maps or real-time features, and store submission. Ask every studio to list these as separate lines.

## Clinics: Where a Booking App Differs From a Coaching App

A clinic booking app differs from a coaching app mainly in how often the patient returns, and most clinics can start with a booking link, not an app. The sources I could use cover coaching institutes and say nothing about clinic apps, so this section is my reasoning from Elyx's pages, not sourced research.

A student uses a coaching app daily for a year, and a patient may book twice a year. Daily use is where an installed app earns its place, so a clinic usually gains more from one-tap booking on a website or WhatsApp. Elyx's Ashirwad Dental redesign gave patients a one-tap WhatsApp booking start on a website, not an app. [Website for Doctors and Clinics in India](/blog/website-for-doctors-india/) covers booking and Google Maps in detail.

Reminders matter for both: Elyx's booking page lists confirmations and reminders by WhatsApp, SMS or email, with easy rescheduling and optional deposits. A clinic app earns its cost when you run many doctors, repeat treatment plans or need patients to see records.

Two cautions. Elyx's dentists page notes that medical and dental councils restrict how clinics may advertise, so approve every claim and photo in a listing. Patient details are personal data, so confirm your obligations with a qualified adviser, because I have not sourced those rules here.

## Choosing a Developer and What the Contract Must Say

The contract matters as much as the quote, because ownership, payment milestones and store accounts decide whether you can leave a developer later. The developer page offers a 12-point checklist, and the points that carry weight are below, as that page's advice and not law.

- **Ownership in writing.** Ask for a clause that all intellectual property belongs to you on delivery. The page says it adapted this from a model agreement on startupindia.gov.in, but I have not read that document.
- **Milestone payments.** Tie each payment to features you have tested, and never pay in full before seeing working software.
- **Accounts in your name.** The repository, store accounts and keys should be yours from day one, with one backup you control.
- **A fix budget.** The page suggests setting aside 10% of total cost for post-launch fixes, a rule of thumb with no method shown.
- **Tax paperwork.** Ask for a GST invoice and check TDS with a chartered accountant.

The page also states that Indian law already vests paid-for code in the client. I would not rely on that, because the same page says elsewhere that you may not own the code unless the contract says so. Before the final payment, test student login, fee payment, results and the notice board on Android and iPhone with a few real students.

## What Usually Goes Wrong

Most coaching app problems start before the first line of code, in a missing scope, a missing contract or parents who never install. The table gives each cause and its fix.

| What goes wrong | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Building before digitising | Fees, attendance and results are not yet tracked in any tool | Digitise one task first with a ready-made tool, as the developer page advises |
| Unlimited feature list | No day-one cut line | Name the user, the problem and three features |
| Parents do not install | App fatigue across many classes | Offer WhatsApp updates and links as well |
| Surprise running costs | Hosting, video, push notifications and retainers left out | Ask for every recurring fee in writing |
| Stuck with one developer | Code and accounts not assigned to you | Written IP clause, accounts in your name |
| Payments launch late | Gateway verification is slow | Apply before the build starts |
| Unprovable claims | Rank or selection claims shown in banners | Publish only results you can document |

## FAQ

### Does a coaching centre need its own app?

Not usually at the start, because ready-made tools and a website cover attendance, fees, messaging and demo-class enquiries for a standard institute. The developer page I read says custom development makes sense only after you have tried those tools and found clear gaps. Offline use, push notifications or daily repeat use are the signs an app is earning its cost.

### What is a coaching institute app?

A coaching institute app is software, usually on Android and iPhone, that handles an institute's batches, tests, attendance, study material, parent updates and fee payments. It tracks progress toward an exam, which a school app does not.

### How much does coaching institute app development cost?

One developer page puts a custom coaching app at ₹2 to 8 lakh upfront plus ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month, as of 19 November 2026, and ready-made platforms at ₹18,000 to ₹50,000 a year. Elyx quotes each project to your requirements, with engagements starting from {{start}}.

### What is the difference between a white-label and a custom coaching app?

A white-label app is a pre-built app with your brand on it, and a custom app is built from scratch around your workflows. The developer page says white-label costs less than custom and takes 4 to 8 weeks including store review. Choose custom only when no product offers the workflow you need.

### Which features should the first version include?

Demo-class booking, a batch timetable with reminders, attendance, fee collection with receipts and parent notices. That is my reading of the sources' feature lists, not a tested standard. Live classes, a test engine and recommendations can follow.

### How long does it take to build a coaching app?

The developer page says custom takes 6 to 9 months, no-code 4 to 12 weeks and white-label 4 to 8 weeks including store review. Elyx's published timeline for a scoped mobile app is {{weeks:mobile}}, agreed in writing before work starts.

### Can parents pay fees by UPI inside the app?

Yes, through a payment gateway such as Razorpay, Cashfree or PayU, which accepts UPI, cards and net banking. Elyx's pages say the gateway handles card details and confirms each payment, and that gateway verification takes time, so apply early.

### Do clinics need an app to take bookings?

Most clinics do not, because patients visit a few times a year and a booking link on a website or WhatsApp covers the job. An app can pay for a clinic with many doctors or repeat treatment plans. This answer is my reasoning from Elyx's pages, not sourced research.

### How do I develop a coaching app step by step?

Write a one-page brief, try a ready-made tool, then choose white-label or custom only for the gaps you can name. Get written quotes with screens, back end, payments and store submission as separate lines, and sign a contract that assigns the code to you.

### How do I open a coaching institute?

This article does not cover registration or licensing, and the research behind it has no primary source on that, so ask a chartered accountant or your local authority. On the digital side, the sources suggest starting with one task, such as fee collection, in a simple tool.

The gap between those tiers comes from scope, not from hourly rates: self-serve sign-up, subscription billing, team roles and an admin panel make up roughly half of a build. The qualification that matters is that every figure here is an asking price from a vendor, so budget the build plus a year of upkeep, and compare quotes line by line.

- **Ranges.** Seven agency pages put a focused MVP between 3 and 45 lakh, and most cluster at 3 to 15 lakh.
- **Where it goes.** Accounts, billing, admin and reporting are about 36 percent of one published 120-day estimate.
- **Cut list.** Usage billing, single sign-on, a public API, a mobile app and real-time dashboards can wait for v2.
- **After launch.** Sources put maintenance at 15 to 25 percent of the build cost, every year.

## SaaS Development Cost India: What Published Ranges Say

Published agency ranges for a SaaS MVP in India run from 3 lakh to 45 lakh, because the word "MVP" covers very different scopes. The table lists every range in the research package, as read on 17 November 2026, with what each publisher says the range includes.

| Publisher | MVP range (lakh) | Timeline | Scope behind the range |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nevatrix | 3 to 8 | About 90 days | Two to four features, sign-up and payment flow |
| Kkeydos | 3 to 8 (richer: 8 to 15) | Not stated | One core problem, few features |
| Appwizards | 4 to 10 | 6 to 10 weeks | Sign-in, one workflow, payment by link, one company |
| Nimbryx | From 6 | 10 to 16 weeks | Multi-tenant, billing and onboarding |
| Voidmatrix | 8 to 15 | 3 to 5 months | Single-tenant, login, dashboard, admin |
| NexaEx | 8 to 18 | Not stated | One workflow, one user role |
| Voidmatrix | 15 to 30 | 5 to 8 months | Multi-tenant with subscription billing |
| Zethic | 15 to 45 | 6 to 12 weeks | "Simple MVP", priced from Indian team rates |

My reading: a one-workflow build with sign-in and a basic payment path sits at 3 to 10 lakh in four of the eight rows, and the range jumps once multi-tenancy and subscriptions are in scope. Zethic's row is the outlier: it starts at 15 lakh for a "simple" MVP, above the lean ranges of every other publisher.

All seven publishers sell software builds, and none states a sample size or method, so read the table as asking prices.

## Why Quotes For The Same SaaS Differ By Five Times

Quotes for the same SaaS idea differ because they price different products, and the cheaper one usually leaves out the half that lets a stranger sign up, pay and get support. Appwizards says roughly half of a typical build is that supporting software, and Zethic says development itself is less than half of total cost once discovery, design, testing and deployment are added.

Appwizards lists seven things that move a SaaS price:

- **User types.** Each role needs its own screens and permission checks.
- **Pricing model.** A flat fee is cheapest; per-seat and usage billing add proration and metering.
- **Tenancy.** How one customer's data stays apart from another's prices everything after it.
- **Integrations.** Each adds a week to a month of work.
- **Real-time features.** Live dashboards cost meaningfully more than pages that load on request.
- **Compliance.** DPDP, GDPR or SOC 2 mean audit logs, encryption and data deletion.
- **Undecided choices.** Changing the pricing model in week nine is the costliest feature of all.

Rates matter less than scope, but the sources disagree on them too: BinaryBits quotes $15 to $25 an hour, SinghJi Tech $25 to $60, and Zethic 1.5 to 4 thousand rupees. Ask which rate a quote assumes. BinaryBits' formula is weeks x people x 40 hours x rate, so 8 weeks with 3 people is 960 hours.

## Scope Tiers: Choose The One That Matches Your Stage

Choose the lean MVP tier if you can create accounts by hand and take payment by link, and the sellable-version tier if strangers must sign up and pay unattended. Appwizards sets out four tiers, summarised below as published in September 2026.

| Tier | What it adds | Range (lakh) | Timeline |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lean MVP | Sign-in, one workflow, basic dashboard, payment by link | 4 to 10 | 6 to 10 weeks |
| Sellable first version | Self-serve sign-up, plans, roles, admin panel, email | 12 to 35 | 10 to 20 weeks |
| Multi-tenant platform | Several workflows, integrations, metering, audit logs, SSO | 35 to 100 | 4 to 8 months |
| Regulated or at scale | DPDP, GDPR or SOC 2 work, data residency, mobile apps | 100 and up | 8 months and up |

Nimbryx's rule is to build for the stage you are at: what wins ten customers is not what you need at a thousand. Nevatrix says 90 days is realistic for one user type and a handful of features, but marketplaces, heavy integrations and regulated products need four to six months even as an MVP.

On tenancy the sources split. Voidmatrix says single-tenant is fine while you validate, while Appwizards and Nimbryx say adding multi-tenancy later is close to a rebuild. My reading: if several companies will share the product, design the separation in week one.

## Auth, Billing And Dashboard Costs, Line By Line

Accounts, billing, an admin view and reporting cost about 36 percent of one published 120-day estimate, and SinghJi Tech prices the same four at $10,000 to $40,000 in total. The table pairs SinghJi Tech's USD ranges with Appwizards' person-day estimates, converted at Appwizards' own rate of 16 thousand rupees a day.

| Component | SinghJi Tech (USD) | Appwizards (days) | Derived (lakh) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accounts, roles, auth | $1,500 to $6,000 | 12 | 1.92 |
| Subscription billing | $3,000 to $12,000 | 15 | 2.40 |
| Admin dashboard | $3,000 to $10,000 | 10 | 1.60 |
| Analytics and reporting | $2,500 to $12,000 | 6 | 0.96 |
| Notifications | $1,500 to $5,000 | 6 | 0.96 |
| Multi-tenant architecture | $5,000 to $20,000 | Not itemised | Not itemised |

The arithmetic: 12 + 15 + 10 + 6 = 43 days, 43 / 120 = 35.8 percent, and 43 x 16 thousand rupees = 6.88 lakh. SinghJi Tech's four rows add to $10,000 at the low end and $40,000 at the high end. The two sources define components differently, so treat the columns as two views, not a match.

Billing is where India adds work. As of the pages read, Appwizards says auto-debit on cards and UPI must be registered with extra authentication under the RBI e-mandate framework, and debits above 15 thousand rupees need authentication each time. Zethic adds a notice at least 24 hours before every debit, and Appwizards notes many Indian B2B products settle on annual invoices paid by UPI or bank transfer.

Invoices need the customer's GSTIN and place of supply, per Appwizards, and NexaEx puts gateway fees near 2 percent on cards. The research holds no RBI circular, so confirm current rules with the RBI and a qualified adviser.

## A Worked Estimate: 120 Person-Days Becomes 19.2 Lakh

A 120-day SaaS build at 16 thousand rupees a day costs 19.2 lakh, and the core workflow is only about a fifth of the days. Appwizards publishes this estimate for a product that schedules technicians and jobs; I grouped its eleven work packages into six and multiplied each by the same day rate.

| Work group | Days | Cost (lakh) |
|---|---|---|
| Discovery and design | 20 | 3.20 |
| Accounts and billing | 27 | 4.32 |
| Core workflow | 25 | 4.00 |
| Admin, reporting, notifications | 22 | 3.52 |
| Testing, security, deployment | 18 | 2.88 |
| One month of fixes | 8 | 1.28 |
| **Total** | **120** | **19.20** |

Check: 20 + 27 + 25 + 22 + 18 + 8 = 120 days, and 120 x 16 thousand rupees = 19.2 lakh. The core workflow is 25 / 120, about 21 percent, so the other 79 percent turns a demo into something a stranger can buy and use with a team. This sits inside Appwizards' 12 to 35 lakh sellable-version band, not the lean one.

## What To Cut From v1

Cut every feature that does not test your one core assumption, and buy the commodity parts instead of building them. Nevatrix tells founders to write that assumption in one sentence, name one target user, list every wanted feature and cross out the rest. Voidmatrix's rule is the same in other words: buy commodity infrastructure, build what differentiates.

| Item | v1 decision | Why, per source |
|---|---|---|
| Login and payments | Buy | Managed auth, Razorpay or Stripe (Nevatrix, Voidmatrix) |
| Usage billing, coupons | Cut | Metering is a small product itself (Appwizards) |
| SSO, public API | Cut | Enterprise buyers ask later (Nimbryx) |
| Mobile app | Cut | Adds $15,000 to $40,000 (SinghJi Tech); web first (NexaEx) |
| Real-time dashboards | Cut | Basic KPIs are cheaper (Voidmatrix) |
| Extra modules | One only | Each is a small product (Nimbryx) |
| Integrations | Defer | A week to a month each (Appwizards) |
| AI features | Defer | $5,000 to $30,000+ (SinghJi Tech) |

Do not cut tenant separation for a B2B product, a failed-payment path, one admin view for support, backups and a staging copy, because cheap quotes omit exactly these. If the AI feature is an agent, the [plain-English guide to AI agents](/blog/what-are-ai-agents/) explains what you would be buying.

## What A SaaS Costs After Launch

A live SaaS has monthly bills from day one, and sources put yearly maintenance at 15 to 25 percent of the build cost. The table gathers every post-launch figure in the research, as read in November 2026.

| Cost | Published figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance | 15 to 25 percent of build a year | Zethic, NexaEx |
| Maintenance, measured | Median 24, 21 and 26 percent in years one to three | Wavect, via BinaryBits |
| Early running costs | 5 to 30 thousand rupees a month | Nevatrix |
| Hosting under load | 2 to 50 thousand rupees a month | NexaEx |
| Payment fees | About 2 percent cards, near zero UPI | NexaEx |

The figures differ because they describe different stages. Plan on Nevatrix's early-stage range until paying customers arrive, then re-estimate. The Wavect benchmark reaches this article through BinaryBits, so treat it as one agency's citation.

## A 12-Month Budget You Can Show An Investor

A 12-month budget for an 8 lakh build comes to between 9.8 and 13.6 lakh before GST, marketing and your own pay. The 8 lakh is my assumed input, chosen from inside the published lean ranges; it is neither a quote nor an estimate for any real product.

| Line | Low (lakh) | High (lakh) |
|---|---|---|
| Build | 8.00 | 8.00 |
| Maintenance, 15 and 25 percent of 8 | 1.20 | 2.00 |
| Running costs, 5 and 30 thousand x 12 | 0.60 | 3.60 |
| **Total** | **9.80** | **13.60** |

The total is 9.8 / 8 = 1.225 and 13.6 / 8 = 1.7 times the build quote, so roughly 23 to 70 percent more. If a quote excludes GST, 18 percent of 8 lakh is 1.44 lakh more. Zethic lists post-launch marketing as another line founders underestimate.

No source says what investors expect an MVP to cost, so this article makes no claim about that. The sources only support showing the build, a year of upkeep and running costs together.

## What Usually Goes Wrong With A SaaS Budget

Most budget failures come from comparing unlike quotes, deferring decisions and stopping the budget at launch. The causes below come from the sources, and the fixes are my reading.

| Mistake | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Choosing the cheapest quote | One omits accounts, billing, admin | Ask for line items and an exclusions list (NexaEx) |
| Tenancy added later | Started single-company to move fast | Decide in discovery (Appwizards, Nimbryx) |
| No failed-payment path | The demo only shows success | Budget retries and reminders (Appwizards) |
| Skipped discovery | Vague requirements | Pay for scoping first (Zethic) |
| No upkeep line | Budget ends at launch | Add 15 to 25 percent a year |
| Built for a thousand users | Day-one scale | Build for your stage (Nimbryx) |
| Built before asking | No customer talks | Show a clickable prototype to five users (Nevatrix) |

BinaryBits adds a test: do not build before speaking to ten would-be customers.

## What To Do Next

Write the one-sentence assumption, price it against the ranges above, then ask two or three teams for line-item quotes. Copy this checklist into your brief, which follows the one BinaryBits recommends:

- [ ] The 3 to 5 jobs a user must do on day one.
- [ ] Every user type and what each can see.
- [ ] How customers pay: plans, trial, per seat or usage.
- [ ] Every outside service the product must talk to.
- [ ] Data to import from spreadsheets or an old system.
- [ ] Web only, or a mobile app too.
- [ ] Who hosts it and who owns the code, in writing.
- [ ] A 12-month budget with upkeep and running costs.

## FAQ

### How much does it cost to build a SaaS platform in India?

Published agency ranges run from about 3 to 10 lakh for a lean MVP and 12 to 35 lakh for a first version you can charge for, per Nevatrix, Kkeydos and Appwizards. Multi-tenant platforms with integrations start around 35 lakh, per Appwizards. The studio's draft SaaS MVP range is {{price:saas}}, indicative and never a quote.

### Can a SaaS MVP be built for under 5 lakh?

Some published ranges start below 5 lakh: Nevatrix and Kkeydos say 3 to 8 lakh and Appwizards says 4 to 10 lakh. Those ranges cover one workflow with sign-in and a basic payment path, not self-serve subscriptions with roles and an admin panel. Check what the quote leaves out.

### How long does it take to build a SaaS product?

A focused MVP takes about 6 to 16 weeks in most published figures, and the studio's draft timeline is {{weeks:saas}}. Appwizards says 6 to 10 weeks for a lean MVP and Nimbryx says 10 to 16 weeks with billing. Nevatrix says marketplaces, heavy integrations and regulated products need four to six months.

### How much does it cost to run a SaaS per month?

Early running costs, covering hosting, tools and payment fees, are 5 to 30 thousand rupees a month per Nevatrix. NexaEx gives 2 to 50 thousand rupees for hosting depending on load. The range is wide because customers, traffic and message volumes decide it.

### What is SaaS cost?

SaaS cost can mean what it costs to build a subscription product or what a customer pays to use one. This article covers the build, in lakh, plus upkeep and running costs. The price you charge customers is a separate decision.

### Can SaaS development costs be capitalised?

US accounting guidance (GAAP) calls for capitalising some software development costs, but SaaS Capital says about 75 percent of SaaS businesses no longer do. SaaS Capital is a US lender and does not cover Indian accounting standards. Ask a chartered accountant how your build should be booked.

### Do SaaS quotes include GST?

It depends on the vendor. The studio's draft ranges exclude 18% GST on software services, while NexaEx says its published ranges are inclusive of GST. Ask every team for the figure with and without tax.

### Is a 6 lakh quote for a SaaS MVP reasonable?

A 6 lakh quote is plausible if the scope is narrow, since Nimbryx lists 6 lakh for a multi-tenant MVP with billing and onboarding and Nevatrix says 3 to 8 lakh. It is low if you need roles, an admin panel and self-serve billing, where Appwizards and NexaEx start higher. Ask for the scope in line items.

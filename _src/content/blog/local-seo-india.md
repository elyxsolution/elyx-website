## Local SEO India: What It Is and How to Do It

Local SEO is the work of making a business visible for searches tied to a place, mainly on Google Maps and in the local results above the ordinary listings. BC Web Wise describes it as targeting people searching within a specific area, as against national SEO. Savit Interactive calls the three map results at the top the local pack and says most clicks land there.

Savit also names three things that decide who appears: proximity, relevance and prominence. That is an agency's description, not Google's wording, but it matches how the work divides. You cannot change where a searcher stands, so you work on relevance (a complete, accurate profile and matching site pages) and prominence (reviews, mentions and links).

The sections below follow the order of the work: profile, listings, reviews, pages, links, then measurement. The studio's own view is that for a business with a physical location, the profile, reviews and consistent address details matter as much as the website. That is practitioner opinion. ## Google Business Profile: Claim It, Verify It, Fill It In

A Google Business Profile is the free listing that puts your address, hours, phone and photos on Google Maps and Search, and Google's documentation says you claim it and verify ownership before you can edit it. Once verified, you can provide or change the address, contact details, business type and photos. Google also advises verifying your website in Search Console, so you can see how Google shows your site.

Complete these fields, using the list that Savit and BC Web Wise both describe:

- **Categories.** One primary category that matches your main service, then secondary ones that are true.
- **Hours.** Include festival and holiday closures.
- **Services and attributes.** List what you actually do, in plain words.
- **Photos.** Real ones of the front, the inside, the team and your work.
- **Posts and Q&A.** Short updates and answers to the questions customers ask you on WhatsApp.

On Posts, Google's own blog shows how a business can share offers and updates where people find it on Search and Maps. Its examples include Ramoji Film City in India, whose team reported a 20% rise in website pageviews after one month of posting. These are hand-picked success stories, not averages.

The same post cites Google/Ipsos Connect research from October 2016: seven in ten customers visit a business or buy after finding information online. The sample was 15,904 adults aged 18 to 64 in India, Australia, Germany, Turkey and the United States, so it is not an Indian figure and it is a decade-old snapshot. ## Citations and NAP Consistency

A citation is a mention of your business name, address and phone number (NAP) on another site, and agencies say consistency across these mentions is a trust signal. Google's pages I read do not state this, so treat it as practitioner opinion. The cheap part is also the safe part: wrong details cost you customers whatever Google does with them.

Savit lists JustDial, Sulekha, IndiaMART and Yellow Pages as directories that matter for Indian businesses. It adds niche ones by trade: Practo for healthcare, Zomato for restaurants and MagicBricks for real estate. White Bunnie names Yelp and Bing Places.

A Reddit thread from March 2026 argues that 300+ citations are the backbone of Indian local SEO. I could not check its case study, so I do not use the number, and my reading is that a dozen correct listings beat hundreds of copies.

Here is an audit on a fictional business, Lakshya IELTS Academy in Mysuru, with made-up listings to show the method.

| Listing | Name shown | Phone shown | Matches profile |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google profile | Lakshya IELTS Academy | One mobile number | Reference |
| Website footer | Lakshya IELTS Academy | Same number | Yes |
| JustDial | Lakshya IELTS Acad. | Landline | No |
| Sulekha | Lakshya Academy Mysuru | Old number | No |
| Facebook page | Lakshya IELTS Academy | Same number | Yes |

Five listings checked, two match, three need fixing, so 3 of 5 (60%) are wrong. Fix them to the exact form on the profile, then keep the list in a sheet with the login and the date you last checked.

## Reviews: How to Ask, and What Not to Do

Reviews help because they show a nearby customer that other people chose you, and Google's own advice lists photos, videos and reviews as ways to prove you are the best choice. Savit and localseoservices.in go further and call reviews a ranking signal, but that is agency opinion. White Bunnie says 72% of consumers are more likely to visit a business with positive reviews, with no study named.

The routine is simple. Ask every customer you served well, ask soon after the visit, and reply to every review in your own words. A short WhatsApp message works because customers already use it:

> Hi [name], thank you for coming to [business] today. If you have a minute, a Google review helps other people nearby find us. Here is the link: [your profile's review link]. Honest feedback is welcome.

Three rules from my reading. Do not script what customers should write. Do not offer money or gifts for five stars. Do not post reviews yourself or buy them. The research holds no text of Google's review policy, so read it before you offer anything in return.

## Location Pages: One Page per Place You Serve

A location page is a page on your own site for one branch or service area, with its own address, hours, map and details, and it earns its place only when the place is real. Savit warns that generic, templated location pages need rewriting with local depth, and White Bunnie recommends a dedicated page for each physical location. The table is my decision rule, not a sourced one.

| Your situation | Pages to build |
|---|---|
| One shop or clinic | Home page plus a contact page with the full address |
| Several branches | One page per branch, each with its own details |
| Service-area business, such as an electrician | A page per city you really serve |
| Places you do not serve | None: do not build them |

Put these on every location page:

- **Name, address, phone.** Identical to the profile.
- **Hours and a map.** An embed plus directions by landmark.
- **Services at this place.** Not copied from another branch.
- **Real photos and staff.** Of this location.
- **A WhatsApp or call button.** Visible on a phone without scrolling.

Savit adds local schema, which is code that tells search engines your name, address and hours, and says it must match what the page shows. Google's guide says to test structured data with the Rich Results Test and to allow a week after a crawl.

## Local Links: Mentions From Sites Your Customers Trust

A local link is a link to your site from another website tied to your area or trade, and agencies treat it as a geographic trust signal that Google's pages I read do not mention. Savit points to local news outlets, regional publications, city blogs and industry directories, and says generic link networks are not the aim. White Bunnie also recommends links from well-regarded local sources.

My suggestions for an Indian small business, which are analysis and not sourced: a trade association or chamber of commerce member page, a supplier's dealer list, a college or event page you sponsor, or a city blog that has covered you. Ask for three, not thirty. A link you could not explain to a customer is probably not worth having.

Ask any agency these questions: who owns the profile and the logins, what is done each month, how results are reported, and how you leave. White Bunnie's page promises "guaranteed results". Treat any guarantee of a position as a red flag, because no one controls Google's results.

## What Usually Goes Wrong

Most local SEO failures come from mismatched details, thin pages and judging by the wrong number, not from missing tricks. The table lists the usual causes from the sources and my reading.

| Mistake | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Profile cannot be edited | Ownership never verified | Complete Google's verification |
| Listings disagree | Several phone numbers and name spellings | Run the audit above |
| Few or no reviews | No routine for asking | Message every satisfied customer |
| Near-duplicate city pages | Pages built for places you do not serve | One page per real location |
| Bought links or reviews | A package that promises volume | Refuse, and read Google's rules |
| Judging by one rank screenshot | Rank differs by neighbourhood | Count calls and enquiries |

## FAQ

### What is local SEO?

Local SEO is the work of making a business show up for searches tied to a place, mainly on Google Maps and in local results. BC Web Wise describes it as connecting businesses with people searching within a specific area. It covers your profile, listings, reviews, site pages and local links.

### How do I do local SEO for my business?

Claim and verify your Google Business Profile, make your name, address and phone identical everywhere, ask real customers for reviews, build one page per real location and earn a few local links. Google's documentation covers the first step and the site basics. The rest is agency practice.

### How much does SEO cost in India?

My research has no sourced price list for local SEO, so I give no figure. Agency pages say pricing depends on industry and competition, and quotes follow the number of locations, your starting point and the scope. Elyx engagements start from {{start}}, and ongoing SEO is quoted after an audit.

### Do I need a website for local SEO?

No, but a site helps. Google's Search Central Blog ran a 2014 video series titled "no website required", and localseoservices.in says a site is not required but recommended. A site gives you location pages, structured data and a place to send customers.

### How long does local SEO take to work?

Agency pages state two to six months, and none shows evidence. localseoservices.in says 2 to 3 months for changes and 4 to 6 for stronger results, and BC Web Wise says 3 to 6. Technical fixes can show sooner than competitive rankings.

### How many citations do I need?

There is no sourced number. A March 2026 Reddit thread argues for 300+, but I could not verify its case study. Start with the directories that fit your trade and keep every detail identical.

### Do Google reviews help local ranking?

Google's own advice says reviews help show customers you are the best choice. Savit and localseoservices.in call them a ranking signal, but that is agency opinion in the pages I read. Real, recent reviews help customers choose you either way.

### Is local SEO different from normal SEO?

Yes, because it targets customers in a specific area rather than a national audience. localseoservices.in lists map pack work, citations and local links as parts of local SEO. The studio's view is that for a shop or clinic, the profile and reviews matter as much as the website.

### Can a business without a shop do local SEO?

Yes, if it serves customers in set areas. localseoservices.in says service-area businesses such as electricians, plumbers and consultants can set a service area on the profile and build listings. Build a page only for cities you actually serve.

A jewellery website India shoppers can buy from does three jobs that Instagram DMs cannot: it lists every piece with a price and photos at a fixed address, it shows the gold rate with a date so a quoted price can be trusted, and it lets the shopper enquire or check out depending on the piece. The honest qualification is that our sources are store homepages and search results, with no figures on jewellery conversion, gold-rate widgets or payment rules. So the catalogue advice is my reading of how three Indian jewellery stores organise their sites, and the gold-rate and checkout advice is a framework to confirm, not a documented standard.

- **Catalogue.** Let shoppers reach a piece by occasion, by type and by collection, and give every piece its own page.
- **Gold rate.** A price that moves daily is a quote with a clock, so show the rate, its source and the time.
- **Enquiry or checkout.** Use checkout when the page price is tomorrow's price, and an enquiry when it is not.
- **Instagram.** Keep posting, but point every post at a page that answers the price question.

## Why Instagram DMs Stop Working as a Shop

Instagram DMs stop working as a shop when most messages ask the same question, price and availability, and a person has to answer each one by hand. The studio's own page for salons describes owners who take most bookings on calls and Instagram DMs and want a simpler, trackable way to fill the calendar. A jeweller's inbox has the same shape.

My reading is that a DM shop has three limits: no price list a customer can read alone, no record of which piece drew the enquiry, and nothing a search engine can index. A public thread in r/IndianBeautyTalks shows the other side, where a shopper asks a seller for a website or Insta page. Buyers ask for both.

### Worked Example: The Cost of Answering "Price?"

Every number here is assumed, so replace each with a count from your own week of DMs.

- 40 DMs a day, 3 minutes each: 40 × 3 = 120 minutes, or 2 hours.
- Half ask only for price and availability: 40 × 0.5 = 20 DMs.
- If a piece page answers them: 20 × 3 = 60 minutes a day saved.

That hour goes back to the conversations that need a person: sizing, bridal sets and custom orders. The studio's salon page adds one caution: live Instagram feeds can slow a site, so show curated images. The [salon article](/blog/salon-website-with-online-booking/) covers that gallery.

## Catalogue Structure: Occasion, Piece Type and Collection

A jewellery catalogue should let a shopper reach a piece three ways, by occasion, by type and by collection, because that is how Attrangi's navigation is built as of 16 November 2026. The table sets the three readable pages side by side.

| Store | How it organises | What you can borrow |
|---|---|---|
| Attrangi | Occasion, piece type, collection, price band | Menus like Bridal, Daily Wear, Under 5K |
| Tarinika | Collections, wedding section, new arrivals | A wedding page and a founder story |
| Voylla | Named collections with long descriptions | A story for each collection |

The table is my grouping of what each page shows. Attrangi's menu lists occasions such as Diwali gifting, bridal and daily wear. It also lists types such as earrings, necklaces, rings and nose rings, and price bands such as under 3K and under 5K. Its listing runs to 1,595 products, and a shopper cannot browse that many through DMs.

Four rules follow, all my reading:

1. **One URL per piece.** A link in a post or a DM should open that piece, not the home page.
2. **Three ways in.** Occasion, type and collection, with price bands as a fourth filter.
3. **Text, not a PDF.** The studio's restaurant page warns that a menu PDF takes ten seconds to open on 4G, and a catalogue PDF behaves the same.
4. **Editable by your team.** The studio's web page describes a CMS so prices, photos and text change without touching code.

Extra product categories are scoped separately in an e-commerce build, so decide your category list before you ask for a quote.

Use this template for each style page:

- **One-line meaning.** Written in your own words, from your karigar or supplier.
- **Occasions.** Bridal, festival, daily wear.
- **Pieces.** Links to every piece in that style.

To find the variants, read a month of your DMs and list the words customers type. Multiple languages are scoped as an option, which matters if your customers search in Kannada, Telugu or Hindi.

## Product Pages: What Every Piece Needs to Show

A product page should answer the questions a DM would, so the shopper never has to ask: weight, material, size, price, stock and delivery. The reviews on Voylla's homepage, read on 16 November 2026, praise pieces for being light, for looking like the photos and for good packaging. None of those facts can be judged without the page showing them.

Put these fields on every piece:

- **Piece code.** A short code the shopper pastes into WhatsApp.
- **Weight and size.** Grams and dimensions, since reviewers mention how light a piece feels.
- **Material.** Metal, purity or plating, and stones.
- **Price, and what it includes.** Say whether making charges and tax are inside it.
- **Photos on a plain background and worn.** Reviewers value a piece that looks as pictured.
- **Stock and delivery.** In stock, made to order, or visit the store.
- **Returns and exchange terms.** Written on the page, not agreed in a DM.
- **One WhatsApp button.** It opens a message with the piece code already typed.

Tarinika shows customers wearing pieces at weddings. Use customer photos only with the customer's permission. For gold, show whatever hallmark and purity details your trade rules require. Our sources do not cover those rules, so confirm them with the Bureau of Indian Standards or your chartered accountant.

## Live Gold-Rate Display: Three Ways and Their Risks

A live gold rate belongs on the site only if every price it touches shows the rate's source and time, because a rate with no clock makes every price a promise you may not keep. Shoppers do search for rates: jewellery rate today in india and gold jewellery price today in india both appear in our keyword suggestions, at low weight. None of the three readable stores shows a rate, because they sell fashion pieces with fixed tags.

| Approach | Choose it when | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed price tag | Fashion, plated or lightweight pieces | Repricing by hand |
| Rate you update daily | Gold priced by weight | A stale rate left up |
| Automatic rate feed | You reprice many pieces daily | Data source and its terms |

The table is my framework, not a sourced benchmark. The studio's web page lists a CMS for changing prices, but no rate-feed integration, so treat a feed as a custom item to scope on a discovery call. Ask where the data comes from and whether you may display it.

Wherever a rate appears, add four lines of text:

1. The source of the rate.
2. The date and time it was set.
3. The purity it refers to.
4. Whether the price is held when the shopper orders.

This article gives no tax or hallmarking advice. Ask a chartered accountant how GST applies to your invoice and making charges.

## Enquiries vs Checkout: A Decision Framework

Choose checkout when the price on the page is the price you will charge tomorrow, and an enquiry when it is not. The figures show where checkout already works. Attrangi's eight listed bestsellers run from ₹2,950 to ₹13,450 on one page, each with an add-to-bag button, and the site offers free shipping on prepaid orders. Tarinika offers 10% off orders above ₹2,500.

Those are fashion pieces with fixed tags. My framework for the rest:

| Piece | Route | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Fashion, fixed price | Checkout | Price holds, parcel ships |
| Silver or plated, fixed price | Checkout | Same, with stock shown |
| Gold priced by weight | Enquiry, then payment link | Rate changes daily |
| Bridal or made to order | Enquiry, then visit or call | Needs a conversation |
| High-value pieces | Reserve, then store visit | Buyer wants to see it |

A hybrid works for many shops. Show the piece and price, offer "Buy" where the price is fixed and "Ask on WhatsApp" where it is not. The studio's e-commerce range includes a catalogue, cart, UPI and card payments, order emails and an admin panel. Confirm payment limits and fees with your gateway before you promise anything to customers.

The site works best when WhatsApp, Instagram and Google Maps all point into it and it points back to them. ## What Usually Goes Wrong on Jewellery Websites

Most jewellery sites fail on trust and upkeep, not on looks. The causes below draw on the studio's build notes where they apply, and the rest, with every fix, is my reading.

| What goes wrong | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Price asked on every post | No price on the page | A page per piece with price |
| Stale gold price | Rate with no timestamp | Source and time beside the rate |
| Checkout on a rate-priced piece | Price changed after payment | Enquiry, then a payment link |
| PDF catalogue | Easy to produce | Text pages, edited in a CMS |
| Slow page | Live Instagram feed, big photos | Curated, compressed images |
| Photos that mislead | Edited or lit unlike the piece | Plain and worn photos, true colour |
| Selling-out banner that is false | Copied from a big store | Say it only if it is true |
| No store page | Online-only thinking | Address, hours, map, phone |

On the false banner: Attrangi's cart carries a message that items are selling out quickly. Whether it is true there, I cannot check. A made-up scarcity line costs trust faster than it earns a sale.

## FAQ

### How do I make a jewellery website?

Make a jewellery website by listing your pieces first, then building one page per piece, three ways to browse and a route to enquire or pay. Add a store page with your address and hours. Choose a template or a custom build once the piece list exists.

### Which jewellery brand is best in India?

This article cannot name a best brand, because the research gives no comparison of brands. It lists who appears in Google results as of 16 November 2026, which is not a quality ranking. Judge a brand by its stated terms and your own visit.

### Should a jeweller show the gold rate on the website?

Show it only if each price it affects also shows the rate's source and time. Shoppers search for the daily rate, so showing it can help. A stale rate does more harm than none.

### Should I take payments online or only enquiries?

Take payments online for pieces whose price holds, and use enquiries for pieces priced on the day's gold rate or made to order. Many shops do both. Confirm payment limits and fees with your gateway.

### Can I just use Instagram instead of a website?

Instagram can stay your showroom, but it cannot hold a price list, a rate with a timestamp or a searchable catalogue. A website adds those. Keep posting, and link each post to its piece page.

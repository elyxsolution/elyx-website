If you run a clinic, salon, café, coaching centre or any business where customers are nearby, you have probably been told you "need a website". That is true eventually. But it is not the first thing to do, and it is not the only thing. Here is the honest comparison.

## What a Google Business Profile does

A Google Business Profile is the listing that appears on Google Maps and in the box of local results when someone searches "salon near me". It shows your name, photos, hours, phone number, directions and reviews. It is free, and you can set it up in a day (verification can take longer).

It is the best tool for being **found** by people who are ready to visit or call. It is not a place to explain your services in depth or to build a brand.

## What a website does

A website is the place you control. It lets you explain each service properly, show real work, answer common questions, publish prices, and collect enquiries through a form or WhatsApp. It is also what Google reads to understand what you do.

It is the best tool for **convincing** people and **converting** them. On its own it will not make you appear in "near me" searches, because Google needs a profile to place you on the map.

## So which first?

**Set up the profile first.** It costs nothing, takes a day and starts producing calls and directions requests quickly. Follow our guide to [ranking on Google Maps](/blog/rank-on-google-maps/).

**Then build the website.** Once customers find you, a good site turns the curious into enquiries. A focused landing page starts at {{start}}, and you can estimate the cost with our [calculator](/tools/website-cost-calculator/).

**Link them together.** Put your website on your profile, and your profile's exact name, address and phone number on your website. Consistency helps both.

## When you can skip one

- **Online-only business, no location:** you may not be eligible for a Business Profile. Put your effort into the website and content.
- **Pure word of mouth, tiny budget:** the profile alone is a sensible start, with a simple WhatsApp number.
- **Selling products online:** you need a website or store; the profile matters if you also have a physical location.

## A simple order of operations

1. Create and verify your Google Business Profile
2. Fill it completely and add real photos
3. Ask your first happy customers for reviews
4. Launch a one-page website with your services, phone, WhatsApp and map
5. Add pages per service as the business grows

## FAQ

### Is a Google Business Profile enough on its own?

For some very small, local businesses, for a while, yes. But you give up control and you cannot explain much. Most businesses outgrow it and add a website.

### Can I have a website without a profile?

Yes, but you will rarely appear on Maps or in local packs without one. For a local business that is a big miss.

### Do I need a profile if I only serve customers at their homes?

Yes, you can set up as a service-area business and hide your address. Google requires an honest setup, so do not list a fake location.

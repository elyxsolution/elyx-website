ChatGPT for website content works as a drafting and editing assistant, not as the writer of record: it produces outlines, headline options and first drafts in seconds, but it does not know your prices, timings, results or voice. Every page therefore needs your facts pasted in and a human fact-check before it goes live. The honest qualification is that this page rests on two practitioner guides and two OpenAI help pages, with no Google document and no test of my own, so the prompts below are templates to try, not proven results.

- **Where it helps.** Outlines, headline options, first drafts, title tags and a second pair of eyes on your own draft.
- **Where it fails.** Anything specific to your business: prices, credentials, results, local rules and the way you talk.
- **The fix.** A five-part prompt, a fact-check pass and a voice sheet, all below.
- **The limit.** ChatGPT can now host a site too, and the OpenAI pages show what it does and does not cover.

## ChatGPT for Website Content: Where It Helps and Where It Fails

## A ChatGPT Prompt for Website Content That Works: Five Parts

A prompt that works names the page, the reader, the facts to use, the format wanted and a check on the result. That is my synthesis of two sources: Elegant Themes says more specific prompts give better results, and OpenAI's own Sites guidance tells users what to share, what to ask for and what to check.

OpenAI's sample prompt also says to use only supplied material, to ask about missing details and to save a version for review. Those three instructions are the best guard against invented facts. The table turns them into five parts, with a fictional example for **Lakshya IELTS Academy, a made-up coaching centre in Mysuru**.

| Part | What to put in | Example (fictional) |
|---|---|---|
| Page and goal | Which page, one action | Home page, goal is a WhatsApp demo-class request |
| Reader | Who, on which device, what they doubt | Working professionals on phones, unsure evening batches fit a job |
| Facts to use | Pasted from your own records | Batch timings, faculty names, fee sheet as I supply it |
| Format | Sections, length, reading level | Hero headline, three benefits, six FAQs, under 400 words |
| Rules and check | Use only my facts, ask when missing, mark guesses | List every claim you were unsure of at the end |

End every prompt with "ask me for anything missing before you write". It turns silent guesses into questions you can answer.

## Prompts for a Home Page, Service Page, FAQ and Meta Description

Each page type needs the five parts, with one extra instruction that suits the page. The patterns below are my templates, adapted from the uses Elegant Themes and Etched Marketing describe, and I have not benchmarked them.

- **Home page.** "Write three hero headlines and a two-line introduction for [business] in [town], for [reader]. Use only the facts below."
- **Service page.** "Draft sections answering: what is included, who it suits, how it works, what it needs from me. Mark any detail I did not supply."
- **FAQ.** "Turn these ten real customer questions into answers of two sentences. Do not add questions I did not list."
- **Title tag and meta description.** "Write five of each. Titles 60 characters or fewer, descriptions 120 to 155, keyword [x], no claims I cannot prove."
- **Review.** "Here is my draft. List unclear sentences and claims that need proof. Do not rewrite it."

Use your own customer questions for the FAQ prompt. The [website copywriting guide](/blog/website-copywriting-tips/) shows how to pull them from your WhatsApp log, and the [website brief template](/blog/website-requirements-template/) lists the facts worth gathering first.

## Editing for Accuracy: A Fact-Check Pass Before Anything Goes Live

Editing for accuracy means checking every claim against a record you hold, because Elegant Themes says fact-checking remains essential and Etched Marketing wants a subject expert to review the copy. You are that expert. Do the pass in this order:

1. **Mark every claim.** Highlight each number, name, date, place, guarantee and comparison.
2. **Match each to a record.** Price list, certificate, invoice, licence or your own calendar.
3. **Delete what has no record.** An unsupported claim is cheaper to cut than to defend.
4. **Hunt placeholders.** Search for square brackets and "example.com". Elegant Themes noticed that ChatGPT filled image links with placeholders.
5. **Read it aloud.** Cut any sentence you would not say to a customer on a call.
6. **Get a second reader** for regulated or legal lines.

The table shows who should verify what, with the reason ChatGPT cannot.

| Claim type | Why ChatGPT cannot know | Check against |
|---|---|---|
| Fees, GST, discounts | It has your price only if you pasted it | Your current price sheet; your accountant for GST wording |
| Timings, address, phone | Not in its training or your prompt | Google Business Profile and your door sign |
| Qualifications, registrations | Cannot verify a licence | The certificate itself |
| Statistics and research claims | Elegant Themes says its knowledge can be outdated | The named primary source, dated |
| Medical or legal advertising | Rules differ by council or body | Your council or professional body |

## Where ChatGPT Content Fails: Seven Mistakes and Their Fixes

ChatGPT content fails in predictable ways, and each has a cause you can design around. The first four rest on the sources, and the last three are my reading.

| What goes wrong | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Generalised, interchangeable copy | Elegant Themes says output can be generalised | Add specifics only you hold |

## Keeping Your Own Voice: A Voice Sheet to Paste Into the Prompt

You keep your voice by giving ChatGPT samples of how you really write and talk, then editing the draft until it sounds like you. None of the pages I read covers voice, so this method is mine and untested, and the before-and-after is an illustration I wrote, not ChatGPT output.

1. **Collect.** Copy five to ten real replies you sent customers on WhatsApp, with names removed.
2. **Mark patterns.** Note sentence length, how you greet, words you favour, words you would never use.
3. **Write the sheet.** Use the table below, and keep it under 150 words.
4. **Paste it** at the start of the chat with two sample replies.
5. **Edit the output** against the sheet, not against your memory.

| Field | Example (fictional, for Lakshya IELTS Academy) |
|---|---|
| Reader | Students and parents, mostly on phones |
| Sentence length | Short, one idea each |
| Language mix | English, with common Kannada words where natural |
| Never use | "Excellence", "world-class", "student-centric" |
| Always state | Batch timing and where the fee sheet is |
| Tone | Direct, warm, like the front desk |

Generic: "We are committed to delivering excellence in IELTS coaching through a student-centric approach." In the owner's voice: "Our evening batch runs 7 to 8:30 pm on weekdays, so you can finish work and still make class." The second line names a fact that ChatGPT could only know if you told it.

## Custom ChatGPT for Your Website: A Chatbot or ChatGPT Sites?

A custom ChatGPT for your website usually means one of two things: a chat box that answers visitors, or ChatGPT Sites, the OpenAI feature that builds and hosts a site from a description. This article covers the second, because it is the one that overlaps with writing your content. As of 20 November 2026, per OpenAI's Help Center, Sites is available to Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise accounts, and new Sites are private until you change access.

So can ChatGPT build a website for you? Yes for simple pages, and OpenAI's examples are resource hubs, dashboards, trackers, forms and calculators. Its guidance still says to review the preview and check the result, including on a phone.

The limits matter for a business site. Per the same pages, card data must be handled by a third-party payment processor, and you remain responsible for fulfilment, refunds, support and taxes. Sites must not process Protected Health Information, so a clinic form that collects health details needs care (my reading of that rule). Restoring an earlier version restores the code, not the database or uploaded files.

| Choose ChatGPT Sites when | Choose a developer when |
|---|---|
| The site is an internal tool, hub or simple page | You need payments, booking or logins that must never fail |
| You can test it and fix it yourself | You want tested forms and a documented handover |
| Losing saved form data would not hurt | You hold patient or customer records |
| You accept hosting on OpenAI's platform | You need your own hosting and a clean exit |

## Worked Example: What a Month of ChatGPT Sites Hosting Uses

A small site with a form database and a custom domain would use roughly 60 to 66 hosting credits a month at 2,000 visits, from OpenAI's proposed rates. OpenAI says hosting is free through 31 December 2026, and that from 1 January 2027 Plus, Pro and Business accounts pay for usage above their allowance. Plus gets 125 credits a month, shared by all your Sites.

The inputs are OpenAI's: 25 credits a month for a Site with storage, 25 for a custom domain, 16 for each 100,000 requests, and typically 30 to 50 requests per page visit. The 2,000 visits are my assumption, so replace them with your own count.

| Line | Calculation | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Site with database | 1 × 25 | 25 |
| Custom domain | 1 × 25 | 25 |
| Traffic, low | 2,000 × 30 = 60,000 requests; 60,000 ÷ 100,000 × 16 | 9.6 |
| Traffic, high | 2,000 × 50 = 100,000 requests; 100,000 ÷ 100,000 × 16 | 16 |
| Monthly total | 25 + 25 + 9.6, up to 25 + 25 + 16 | 59.6 to 66 |

The allowance leaves 125 − 50 = 75 credits for traffic, or 75 ÷ 16 × 100,000 = 468,750 requests. At 50 requests a visit that is 9,375 visits, and at 30 it is 15,625. The pages I read give no rupee rate for credits, domain registration costs extra, and building or editing counts against separate Codex usage, so the real monthly bill is not something I can state.

## FAQ

### Can I use ChatGPT for my website?

Yes, as a drafting and editing aid, provided you supply your own facts and check every claim before publishing. Elegant Themes and Etched Marketing both say human review stays necessary. Treat the output as a first draft.

### Can ChatGPT create a website for me?

For simple sites, yes: OpenAI's ChatGPT Sites builds and hosts a site from a written description. It is available on Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise accounts as of 20 November 2026, and new Sites start private. Payments, patient data and backups carry limits, listed above.

### How do I use ChatGPT for website content writing?

Give it a page, a reader, your pasted facts, a format and a check, then edit what comes back. The five-part table above is the template. Always ask it to flag anything it guessed.

### Will Google penalise ChatGPT content?

The sources I read do not show a penalty for AI use itself, only for unhelpful, inaccurate or duplicated pages. Etched Marketing, a practitioner, makes that case, and my research has no Google document. Check Google Search Central's current guidance before you publish.

### Does ChatGPT own my content?

I cannot confirm this from the sources I read, because they do not cover OpenAI's terms of use. Read the terms in force on the day you publish, or ask a lawyer. Ownership of the site itself between you and a developer is a separate question.

### What kind of website is ChatGPT?

ChatGPT is a web and desktop chatbot from OpenAI that generates human-like text. Elegant Themes describes it as an AI conversational agent. It has also gained a feature, Sites, that builds websites.

### Is ChatGPT free for building a website?

Not fully. OpenAI lists Sites for Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise, and hosting is free through 31 December 2026, with usage charges proposed from 1 January 2027. Elegant Themes says the free chat version has code limits, which I did not test.

### How do I keep ChatGPT copy sounding like me?

Paste a voice sheet and two real customer replies at the start, then edit against that sheet. This is my method, not a sourced or tested one. Reading each page aloud catches what the sheet misses.

### How do I fact-check ChatGPT website copy?

Mark every claim, match it to a record you hold and delete anything unsupported. Elegant Themes says fact-checking is essential. Have a second person read regulated lines.

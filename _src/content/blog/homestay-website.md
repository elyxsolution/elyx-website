A homestay website wins direct bookings when a guest who found you on an OTA (online travel agency, such as MakeMyTrip or Booking.com) or on Google can check dates, see the price and pay a deposit on your own page, without a platform taking a cut. The qualification that matters is that a website does not fill rooms by itself: it converts guests you already attract, and it only saves money if your calendar stays in sync with every OTA you keep. ## Homestay Website vs OTA Listing: Where the Commission Goes

An OTA keeps a percentage of every booking it sends you, while a booking taken on your own homestay website costs you only the tools and payment fees you choose to pay for. The vendor page we read, Homeyhuts, lists losing commission on every OTA booking among the problems it sells against, and advertises zero commission on direct bookings. That zero describes the vendor's own cut, not your payment gateway's fee, so ask both what they charge.

Here is the arithmetic, using assumed inputs. A two-night stay at ₹3,200 a night is ₹6,400. At an assumed 15 percent commission, the OTA keeps ₹6,400 × 0.15 = ₹960. At an assumed 2 percent gateway fee on a direct booking, you pay ₹6,400 × 0.02 = ₹128. The saving is ₹960 − ₹128 = ₹832 per booking you move from an OTA to your own site.

| Bookings moved to direct each month | Saving a month (× ₹832) | Saving a year (× 12) |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | ₹1,664 | ₹19,968 |
| 5 | ₹4,160 | ₹49,920 |
| 10 | ₹8,320 | ₹99,840 |

Neither 15 percent nor 2 percent is a sourced rate. Read your own OTA statement and your gateway's fee page, then rerun the sum. To see when a site pays for itself, divide its written quote by your per-booking saving: a quote of N rupees needs N ÷ 832 moved bookings at these inputs.

## Homestay Booking Websites: Engine, Channel Manager or WhatsApp

You can take direct bookings through a WhatsApp enquiry, a booking engine on your site, or a channel manager that also syncs your OTA listings, and the right pick depends on rooms and listings. The risk to design around is a double booking: the same night sold on two platforms. Homeyhuts' page frames its real-time sync as the fix and contrasts it with the delays of calendar-file (iCal) syncing.

This is my decision framework, built from that page and the studio's own facts.

| Choose this | When | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| WhatsApp enquiry button | One to three rooms, one OTA at most, guests already message you | Dates and deposits tracked by hand |
| Booking engine on your site | You want guests to see availability and pay a deposit themselves | It does not talk to OTAs unless you add that |
| Channel manager plus booking site | Two or more OTAs, or several rooms and room combinations | A monthly fee, and claims to test before you pay |
| Custom-built booking | Rules no tool handles, such as packages, seasons or group blocks | Highest cost and the longest build |

Homeyhuts also describes selling one property as single rooms, room combinations or the whole villa without conflicts, which is the case a resort with cottages will meet. Its page reports a 28 percent average revenue growth in the first 120 days, 2.5 times more direct booking revenue within the first year and 64 percent average occupancy. Those are one vendor's marketing figures with no method shown, so treat them as claims to test, not forecasts.

## Homestay Website Design: The Sections a Guest Needs

A homestay website design works when a guest can answer five questions in under a minute: what the rooms are, what they cost, where the place is, who the hosts are and how to book. The studio's industry pages for restaurants, clinics and salons share a pattern: a hero with one action, the offer, a gallery, trust details and a closing block with map, hours and WhatsApp. The order below adapts it and is my recommendation.

1. **Hero.** One strong photo, the place name, the town, and a "Check dates" or "Book on WhatsApp" button.
2. **Rooms and tariff.** Each room with a photo, sleeps-how-many, bed type and a price for a typical night.
3. **What is included.** Meals, kitchen access, parking, Wi-Fi, and what costs extra.
4. **Location.** A map, the road approach and the nearest town or station, in plain words.
5. **Host and reviews.** A short note from the host and guest comments you have permission to show.
6. **House rules and cancellation.** Check-in time, deposit and refund terms, written in advance.
7. **Book.** Date picker or WhatsApp button, repeated at the bottom.

The text we extracted from Homestays of India includes template code that builds a WhatsApp message starting with "Hi" and the property's name, so each property page appears to open a pre-filled chat. That saves the guest typing and tells you which room they meant.

## UPI Payments on a Homestay Website

UPI deposits suit Indian guests, but none of the five pages we read documents UPI, so confirm it with your booking tool or payment gateway before you promise it. MakeMyTrip says its payment gateway is secure, and Homeyhuts says its direct booking site accepts secure online payments and tracks payment status. Neither names UPI in the text we read.

What the studio states is narrower and first-hand: it can add UPI and card payments through a payment gateway on a build, and treats it as a separate option in the scope. A simple deposit flow looks like this, and it is my design, not a documented standard.

1. The guest picks dates and the site holds them for a short, fixed time.
2. The guest pays a deposit by UPI, and the booking is confirmed on screen.
3. A WhatsApp message goes out with directions, check-in time and house rules.
4. The balance is paid at check-in, and the refund terms you showed at step 1 apply.

If your tariff attracts GST, ask your CA what the invoice must show and who raises it. The [salon booking article](/blog/salon-website-with-online-booking/) walks through the same UPI-deposit question for another trade, and the same caution applies: ask before you promise.

## What Usually Goes Wrong With Homestay Websites

Most homestay sites fail from a stale calendar, a missing next step or heavy photos, not from design. The fixes below are my reading.

| What goes wrong | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Double booking | The same night is open on an OTA and your site | Use one calendar or a channel manager, and test it with a dummy booking |
| Guests ask the same questions | Rooms, meals and rules are missing | Put them on the page, and answer the rest on WhatsApp |
| "No commission" disappoints | Gateway and software fees were never counted | Add them to the sum above |
| Slow page on 4G | Photos exported at full size | Compress, and measure on a phone |
| Details differ across places | Site, Profile and OTA show different phones or addresses | Fix once, then copy |
| Deposit disputes | Refund terms appear after payment | Show terms before the guest pays |

## FAQ

### How do I create a homestay website?

Start with a one-page site showing rooms, tariff, location, house rules and a WhatsApp button, then add online booking once you run more than a few rooms or OTAs. The section order above is a practical sequence.

### Is a homestay website legit?

A homestay website is legit when its details can be checked outside the site: an address on Google Maps, a working phone number and a registration where your state requires one. Kerala Tourism, for example, publishes a list of accredited providers.

### Is homestay legal in India?

Rules for running a homestay are set by each state, so there is no single answer, and this article gives none for Karnataka. Ask your state tourism department and a qualified professional before you advertise.

### Can a homestay website really reduce OTA commission?

A homestay website reduces commission only on the bookings that move from an OTA to your own page, and only after you subtract your payment and software costs. At the assumed inputs above, each moved booking saves ₹832. Your own statements decide the true figure.

### What is the best homestay website template?

There is no single best template, because a template is a design and the booking calendar, deposits and OTA sync are separate pieces. Pick one only after checking how bookings and payments will work.

### Do I need a booking engine for a small homestay?

A homestay with one to three rooms and at most one OTA can start with a WhatsApp button and a manual calendar. A booking engine pays off when guests keep asking which dates are free, and a channel manager once you sit on two or more OTAs.

### Can guests pay by UPI on my homestay website?

Yes, if your payment gateway or booking tool supports UPI, but none of the vendor pages we read states it. Confirm UPI in writing with the vendor before you put it on the page.

### How do I get my homestay on Google?

Claim and complete your Google Business Profile first, with the same name, address, phone and website as your site. Whether your rates can also appear in Google's hotel results depends on your booking tool, so ask the vendor in writing.

### How many photos does a homestay website need?

There is no sourced number, but cover each room, the food, the view, the host and the approach road, as in the shot list above. Compress every image, because the studio's care page warns that a 6 MB upload can slow a page.

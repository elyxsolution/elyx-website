## The five things that move the price

**1. Size.** The number of pages, screens or modules. A five-page site and a fifty-page catalogue are different projects, even if they look similar on the homepage.

**2. Features.** Online booking, UPI and card payments, user accounts, admin dashboards and integrations each add real engineering work. A contact form is cheap. A booking system with reminders is not.

**3. Content.** Who writes the copy and sources the photos? If you supply them, the price drops. If you want us to write for you, it rises. The single biggest cause of late projects is content that arrives late, so be honest about this one.

**4. Speed.** A rush timeline means reshuffling the calendar. A good studio tells you the premium up front instead of hiding it.

**5. Care after launch.** Hosting, security updates, backups and small edits are a monthly plan, separate from the build. A site with no one responsible for it decays quietly.

## What is usually *not* in the quote

Read the fine print for the items that turn a cheap quote into an expensive project:

- Domain and hosting (usually a few thousand rupees a year, paid to the provider)
- Premium plug-ins, fonts, stock photos or paid tools
- Copywriting and photography
- Changes after the agreed revision rounds
- GST at 18%

## How to compare two quotes

Put them side by side and check four things. First, are the **deliverables** named page by page, or is it just "5-page website"? Second, what is the **timeline**, and what happens if it slips? Third, who **owns** the code, the design files and the domain at the end? Fourth, how are **payments** split? We work on 50% to begin and 50% on final delivery, which keeps both sides honest.

The cheapest quote is rarely the cheapest outcome. A site that loads slowly on mobile, hides your phone number or cannot be edited by your own team costs you enquiries every month.

## FAQ

### Does the price include GST?

No. Prices on this site and in these ranges are exclusive of GST, which is currently 18% on software services, and it is added to the invoice.

### Why do some agencies quote ₹5,000 for a website?

Usually because the quote is for a template with your logo dropped in, with no custom design, no speed tuning and no support. That can be fine for a placeholder. It is rarely enough if the website is how customers find you.

### Can I pay in stages?

Yes. We work on 50% upfront and 50% on final delivery, covered by a written statement of work with scope, deliverables, timeline and revision rounds.

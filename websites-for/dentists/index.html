Websites for dentists & clinics

A clinic website that fills appointment slots.

A good clinic website does four things: shows your treatments clearly, builds trust with real photos, makes booking a one-tap WhatsApp message, and appears on Google Maps. Elyx builds clinic sites from ₹9,999, with landing pages live in 1–2 weeks.

Get a quote WhatsApp us

Who this is for: Clinic owners with 1–3 chairs who want more appointments from local searches.

What your site needs

What a site for dentists & clinics actually needs.

01

WhatsApp and call booking

Patients want to book in seconds. A sticky "Book on WhatsApp" and call button on every page beats a long form.

02

Treatment pages

One page per treatment (implants, braces, root canal, whitening) so you can rank for what patients actually search for.

03

Trust: doctors and clinic photos

Real photos of the clinic and the team, qualifications, and a gallery. This is what turns a nervous visitor into a booking.

04

Google Maps and local SEO

Map embed, directions, opening hours and local schema, tied to your Google Business Profile, which is where "dentist near me" is won.

05

Fast on a phone

Most clinic searches happen on mobile, often in a hurry or with a toothache. Speed is part of the service.

06

Clear fees and FAQs

Answering "how much does it cost" and "does it hurt" on the page saves calls and earns trust.

Questions

Common questions, honest answers.

How much does a dental clinic website cost in India?

A focused clinic landing page starts at ₹9,999. A fuller multi-page site with treatment pages, a gallery and a CMS costs more and is quoted in writing after a short call. See how we quote.

Can patients book through WhatsApp?

Yes. Every page can have a click-to-chat WhatsApp button that opens a pre-filled message to your clinic number. This was part of the Ashirwad Dental redesign.

Will the site help me show up on Google Maps?

The site supports it with local schema and consistent address details, but Maps rankings are driven mainly by your Google Business Profile. We help set that up and optimise it.

Can I add before-and-after photos and patient reviews?

Technically yes, but council rules on clinic advertising apply, so we only publish what you are comfortable is compliant.

I already have a website. Can you redesign it?

Yes. We audit it, keep what ranks, and rebuild the rest. Redesigning without losing your Google rankings is something we plan for from the start.

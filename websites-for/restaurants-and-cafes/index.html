Websites for restaurants & cafés

A restaurant website people open before they walk in.

A restaurant website has one job: get someone from "where should we eat" to your door or your WhatsApp. That means a fast mobile menu, photos, timings, directions and a reservation or order button. Elyx builds them from ₹9,999.

Get a quote WhatsApp us

Who this is for: Restaurant, café and cloud-kitchen owners who want more direct bookings and less dependence on aggregator apps.

What your site needs

What a site for restaurants & cafés actually needs.

01

A menu that loads instantly

A real, searchable menu page, not a PDF that takes ten seconds to open on 4G.

02

Table booking and WhatsApp orders

Reservation request, WhatsApp click-to-order and a call button, so guests reach you in one tap.

03

Photos and atmosphere

A gallery and short copy that conveys the place: food, interiors and what makes you different.

04

Google Maps and timings

Accurate hours, holiday notes, directions and map embed, synced with your Google Business Profile.

05

FSSAI and trust details

Your FSSAI licence number and contact details displayed clearly, as food businesses are expected to show them.

06

UPI and online ordering (optional)

If you want direct ordering to reduce aggregator commissions, we can add a simple order flow with UPI payment.

Questions

Common questions, honest answers.

How much does a restaurant website cost?

A one-page site with menu, photos, map and a WhatsApp button starts at ₹9,999. Direct online ordering is a larger build and is quoted after a call.

Can customers order directly on WhatsApp?

Yes. We add click-to-chat buttons with a pre-filled message, so ordering takes a tap and no commission is paid to a platform.

Can I update the menu myself?

Yes. We can set up a simple CMS so you can change dishes, prices and photos without a developer.

Will it show on Google Maps?

Maps depends mainly on your Google Business Profile. We help set that up and match the details on your site.

Do you build online ordering?

Yes, as a larger project. We have built a full e-commerce platform with payments for Malnad Stories, and the same approach applies to food ordering.

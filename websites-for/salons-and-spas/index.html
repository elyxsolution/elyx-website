Websites for salons & spas

A salon website that books the chair for you.

Salon and spa customers choose by photos, prices and how easy it is to book. A good site shows your services and prices, a gallery of your work, and a booking button on every screen. Elyx builds them from ₹9,999.

Who this is for: Salon, spa and studio owners who take most bookings on calls and Instagram DMs and want a simpler, trackable way to fill the calendar.

Service menu with prices

Questions

Common questions, honest answers.

How much does a salon website cost?

A focused one-page site with services, prices, gallery and a booking button starts at ₹9,999. Online scheduling with calendars is quoted after a short call.

Can clients book online?

Yes, through a booking widget or a WhatsApp flow, depending on how you work today and how many staff you have.

Can I show my Instagram posts?

We can build a fast gallery of your best work on the site. Live Instagram feeds can slow a site down, so we recommend curated images.

Can I update prices and offers myself?

Yes. We add a simple CMS and a short walkthrough so you can edit prices and offers any time.

Will it help new customers find me on Google?

Yes, mainly through local SEO and your Google Business Profile. We set both up together.
